Malay is treated in the project as a major diplomatic and trading language of the Brunei-centered maritime world.

Ache’s long residence at the Brunei court and later command make working fluency a strong project inference, but the archive does not currently have a primary sentence explicitly documenting his proficiency.

This practice matters narratively because language can mark status, belonging, negotiation, misunderstanding, and Ache’s widening political world.
