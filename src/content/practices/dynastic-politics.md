Kinship is one of the project’s central political mechanisms.

Ache’s position links Maynila, Brunei, Tondo, marriage, succession, and competing claims to authority. Some relationships are directly documented while others depend on later reconstruction, so the story bible keeps named historical evidence separate from novel-canon identifications.

In the novel, kinship should create obligations, leverage, rivalry, inheritance problems, and alliance opportunities rather than functioning as background genealogy.
