A fictional composite crisis created for the novel's Book III climax.

Captains and local power-holders resist parts of Ache's developing system of dues, naval obligations, appointments, records, and delegated authority. Their resistance disrupts the political order around Manila Bay and tests whether Ache's institutions can survive opposition.

The event is **novel fiction**, not a claim that a documented rebellion of this form occurred.

Its story purpose is to test the state Ache has built before the separate historical Spanish crisis begins.
