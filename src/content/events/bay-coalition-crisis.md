## What happens in the published novel

In Book III Chapters 9–10, traders, fish sellers, carriers, a boat owner and the keeper confront a damaged landing and rumors that a universal passing charge has been agreed. The cord owner wants paid watchers. Sela, Laman, Hiran and the clay-weight trader have different commercial needs and refuse to let a proposed measure become a compulsory levy without their agreement. Raya keeps the unsafe step closed until she can test a replacement, and Dala retains control of her fishing path.

The three knots gather *requests*, not a binding political coalition. The keeper records distinct offers, objections and witness accounts. In Chapter 10 the timber is purchased and a conditional passage is tested; one part of Sela's fish load reaches a buyer while another arrives too late. No one adopts a fee for every boat. The cord owner continues to serve traders at a rival landing. Ache gains a limited ability to work and answer for his own promises, without acquiring control over other people's boats, shores or labor.

**This is a local fictional commercial and governance dispute.** The published chapters do not depict captains rebelling against a Maynila-wide state, a royal naval levy, hereditary appointments, a bay-wide coalition or the overthrow of Ache's authority.

## Development-history boundary

The older working concept called *Bay coalition crisis* imagined captains and local power-holders resisting Ache's expanding dues, naval obligations, appointments and delegated authority. That political rebellion **was not published** in Chapters 9–10. The original entity ID and aliases are retained to preserve existing Universe links and development-history references, while the name, character metadata and account above describe what the reader actually encounters.

All specific participants, bargaining terms, record strips and repairs are fictional. No historical source establishes this dispute as an event in sixteenth-century Maynila. The documented Spanish encounters occur later in the two bonus chapters and must not be backdated into this local core-book conflict.
