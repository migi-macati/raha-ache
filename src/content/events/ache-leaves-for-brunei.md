Aganduru Moriz says Ache told his mother he intended to go to Brunei to ask his grandfather Siripada for support in recovering Maynila's lands. His mother tried to dissuade him, then gave permission and gold.

## Novel canon

Book I expands this journey into a flight through the Manila Bay–Mindoro–Palawan corridor, with Pasay and Bulkan accompanying Ache and a Tondo force pursuing them.
