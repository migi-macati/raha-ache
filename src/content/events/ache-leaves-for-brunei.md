## Retrospective account

Aganduru Moriz says Ache told his mother he intended to go to Brunei to ask his grandfather Siripada for support in recovering Maynila's lands. His mother tried to dissuade him, then gave permission and gold. This is a seventeenth-century report of an earlier episode; the original Spanish passage and the departure's precise date have not been independently verified. The narrative's specific conversation and gift of gold should not be presented as contemporary eyewitness testimony.

## Novel reconstruction

Book I expands the journey into a pursued maritime flight through the Manila Bay–Mindoro–Palawan corridor.

Sulad is Ache's current principal companion in this version. The route, pursuit, dangers, and supporting cast around the journey are novel reconstruction unless separately sourced.
