Pigafetta says the Luzon prince had come from the city of Loue after destroying and sacking it because the city refused obedience to the king of Brunei and instead obeyed the king of Java Major.

Aganduru gives a different emphasis: Ache sought prizes and power before returning to Maynila with the Bruneian force.

The reported attack on Loue appears in Pigafetta's account of what the captured Luzon commander had done, rather than in his own eyewitness account of the campaign. Pigafetta does not name that commander Ache. The campaign report is early evidence; attributing the campaign specifically to Ache remains a cross-source inference, and the modern location of Loue is unresolved.
