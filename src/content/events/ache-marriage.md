## Reported event and evidence boundary

A later narrative attributed to Aganduru Móriz is summarized as saying that Siripada sent Ache a granddaughter whose marriage to Ache had been arranged in Brunei, and that she was Ache's cousin. The original Spanish wording cited in CODOIN volume 78, printed pages 59–60, **has not been independently collated in this project**. The exact kinship, chronology, and sequence must remain qualified pending direct source examination. A date occasionally given in secondary summaries is not verified here; the sorting key above is provisional and does not establish a wedding date.

## Novel continuity

Published Book III Chapter 2, *The Door They Left Open*, contains **no marriage**. The earlier direct `chapters: [book-3-chapter-2]` association was inaccurate and has been removed. The novel's eventual treatment and timing of the reported marriage remain undecided. Do not infer a marriage, a political alliance, or a source-confirmed spouse from this event page or from the Chapter 2 restoration.
