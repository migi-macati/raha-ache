Pigafetta records the expedition capturing a junk whose commander was the son of the king of Luzon and captain-general of the king of Brunei. The commander had come from Loue after attacking that city.

Aganduru Moriz identifies the prince with the future Ladyang Matanda and gives a fuller story of his purpose, capture, release, and return.

## Aganduru sequence

Ache was returning toward Maynila with a large Bruneian force.

The European ships fought the approaching force and captured Ache's flagship.

After learning that Ache was Siripada's grandson, del Cano released him, returned his ship and property, and declined the offered gold.

Ache supplied a Makassarese pilot, Pazeculan, for the voyage toward the Moluccas.

## Source difference

Pigafetta says the pilot Giovan Carvaio released the captured captain for gold. Aganduru says del Cano released Ache freely in gratitude for Siripada's treatment of the expedition. Both versions remain visible rather than being harmonized.
