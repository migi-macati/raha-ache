Aganduru Moriz says Ache's force reached Maynila successfully, recovered his lands, forced the king of Tondo back, and plundered Tondo settlements.

He then negotiated peace. Aganduru says the Tondo rulers submitted as they had before and Ache became a feared king and lord.

The scale and constitutional meaning of this claimed submission should not be projected into modern territorial sovereignty without further evidence.
