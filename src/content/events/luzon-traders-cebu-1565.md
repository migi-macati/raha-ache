Seven or eight Moros from Luzon, led by Magomat, are recorded trading with Legazpi's expedition in Cebu.

An experienced Moro pilot told Legazpi that two Luzon junks were in Butuan trading gold, wax, and slaves.

This evidence describes Luzon's trading network before Legazpi had direct contact with Ache.
