Legazpi returned to Manila Bay in 1571.

Riquel records Raja Ache the Old and Raja Soliman the Young as lords and principals of Manila, and Sibunao Lacandola as principal of Tondo.

Ache and Lakandula pursued accommodation with Legazpi while Sulayman's position was more resistant.
