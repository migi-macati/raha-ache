Martín de Goiti's force reached Maynila in 1570. Negotiations with Ache and Sulayman broke down and fighting followed.

Spanish forces overran and burned the fortified settlement before withdrawing rather than permanently occupying Manila that year.

Ache's exact conduct during the fighting is less clearly recorded than Sulayman's resistance.
