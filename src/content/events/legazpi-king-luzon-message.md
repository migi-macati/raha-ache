Legazpi's expedition learned from regional traders that Luzon was a large northern trading island. Project research notes record a message sent to an unnamed "king of Luzon" expressing Legazpi's desire to meet him.

Modern historical reconstruction identifies this ruler with Ache.

The primary wording does not itself name Ache.
