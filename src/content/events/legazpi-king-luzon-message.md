## Documented event

Legazpi's expedition learned from regional traders that Luzon was a large northern trading island. Project research notes preserve a message sent to an unnamed "king of Luzon" expressing Legazpi's desire to meet him.

## Historical identification

Modern historical reconstruction identifies this ruler with Ache. The primary wording currently preserved in the project does not itself name Ache.
