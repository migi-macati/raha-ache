Aganduru Moriz, writing in the seventeenth century, reports that Ache's father died when Ache was newly born and that his mother then governed Maynila. The precise timing and succession circumstances are not independently corroborated by the contemporary accounts currently cited in this project.

Book I Chapter 2 remembers Ache's absent father; the death itself occurs before the novel's opening, not within that chapter.
