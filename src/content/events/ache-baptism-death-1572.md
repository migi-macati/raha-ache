## Documented event

Ache died in 1572 after becoming Christian.

The 1572 succession account says he had no children by his legitimate wife who could inherit under the rule being described; his property therefore descended to a legitimate nephew.

## Identity uncertainty

The succession passage preserved in the project does not name that nephew. Sulayman's relationship to the succession and the later Bangkusay traditions remain a separate reconstruction problem.
