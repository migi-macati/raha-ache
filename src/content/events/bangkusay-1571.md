## Documented event

A resistance fleet drawn from communities around Manila Bay and Pampanga fought the Spanish at Bangkusay.

## Identity uncertainty

Project research notes associate Bambalito of Macabebe with the resistance and preserve later identifications linking the dead leader to Tarik Sulayman.

The relationship among Bambalito, Tarik Sulayman, and Rajah Sulayman of Maynila is not settled. The event is documented; those personal identifications are not treated as equally certain.
