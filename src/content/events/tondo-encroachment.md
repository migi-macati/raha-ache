A later narrative attributed to Aganduru Móriz describes an unnamed ruler of Tondo, reportedly Ache's cousin, exerting pressure on Maynila while Ache's mother governed. It also relates Ache's dissatisfaction and his eventual decision to seek support from his grandfather in Brunei.

**Evidence boundary:** The original Spanish passage has not yet been independently checked. This is retrospective testimony, not a contemporary record of Ache's youth. The ruler's identity and the exact form of Tondo's pressure remain uncertain. The ruler must not be identified as the Sibunao Lacandola documented in 1571 without separate evidence.

**Novel reconstruction:** Book I portrays the pressure through fictional boat tallies, labor disagreements, commercial decisions, and Ache's conflict with his mother. These scenes are inventions, not documented incidents.
