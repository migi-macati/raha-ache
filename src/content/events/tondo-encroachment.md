Aganduru Moriz says the ruler of Tondo, Ache's cousin, gradually entered Maynila's lands while Ache's mother governed.

Ache objected to being treated as a younger brother and later resolved to seek his grandfather's support in Brunei.

The identification of this unnamed Tondo ruler with Lakandula is secondary reconstruction.
