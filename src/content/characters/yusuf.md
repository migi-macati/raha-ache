Yusuf is a merchant who hires Ache to witness cargo counts in Book II, Chapter 5. Before leaving Burné, he leaves behind two bundles subject to competing claims. At the eastern landing, six rolls under his account encounter a second, older dispute.

Yusuf accepts an expensive temporary arrangement: five rolls are delivered to Karim and one stays under independent watch while earlier claims are heard. He pays for delay and carrying, but does not accept responsibility for every alleged past loss or pay for Ache's return after Ache gives away his provisions. His goods and contracts are invented for the novel.
