Idris is a fictional young cargo carrier introduced in Book II, Chapter 1, *The Other End of the Cord*. A merchant with a basket of imported ceramic bowls alleges damage and withholds Idris's agreed payment. Idris does not accept a reduced payment as settlement. The dispute over the basket's condition and custody ends without establishing who caused the breakage.

Later, Idris performs separate work for the receiving house and receives the payment agreed for that new job. **The merchant's earlier wage remains unpaid.** Ache eventually asks his name after spending much of the day speaking about the carrier's hands, possible fault and compensation without addressing him personally.

Idris has his own claim and work choices; he does not become Ache's dependent or proof of Ache's moral success. The character and dispute are fictional, not attested historical persons or legal procedures.
