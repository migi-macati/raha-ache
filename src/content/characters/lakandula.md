Riquel directly names **Sibunao Lacandola** as principal of Tondo in 1571.

## Reconstruction

A later account attributed to Aganduru Móriz describes an **unnamed** ruler of Tondo as Ache's cousin during his youth and associates him with pressure on Maynila during Ache's mother's regency. The original Spanish passage and its underlying provenance have not been verified here. Alfonso (2016) conditionally proposes Malangsi for the unnamed cousin, relying in part on disputed genealogical evidence. The identification of that early ruler with the Sibunao Lacandola documented in 1571 is **not established**.

## Novel use

Keep the early Tondo rival unidentified in Ache's youth. Lakandula may appear in the later, historically documented 1571 setting, but do not transfer his name, kinship or motives backward to the early rivalry without explicit fictional framing and separate justification.

## Human contradictions (fictional characterization)

If used as a character in the 1571 storyline, give him independent motives and constraints. Do not present invented private attitudes toward Ache as documented biography or make him a secret mastermind behind every harbor abuse.
