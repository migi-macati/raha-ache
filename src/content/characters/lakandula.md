Riquel directly names **Sibunao Lacandola** as principal of Tondo in 1571.

## Reconstruction

Aganduru Moriz describes an unnamed ruler of Tondo as Ache's cousin during Ache's youth and says this ruler encroached on Maynila during the regency of Ache's mother.

Secondary reconstruction commonly identifies that unnamed cousin with the Lakandula known from 1571. The story bible preserves that identification as a strong inference rather than a direct naming by Aganduru.

## Novel use

The current story framework can use Lakandula as Ache's Tondo kinsman and political rival, but scenes built on the youthful rivalry remain fictional reconstruction around the historical identification.
