Ruler of Tondo and kinsman of Ache.

Riquel records Sibunao Lacandola as principal of Tondo in 1571. Secondary historians commonly identify the Tondo cousin in Aganduru Moriz's account of Ache's youth with Lakandula.

## Novel canon

The early outline makes the Tondo cousin Ache's principal political rival during Book I and expands the rivalry into a pursuit south from Manila Bay.

## Uncertainty

The identification of Aganduru's unnamed Tondo cousin with the later Lakandula is a historical reconstruction.
