Leader associated in Spanish accounts and later reconstructions with the resistance that culminated at Bangkusay in 1571.

## Uncertainty

Bambalito, Tarik Sulayman, and Rajah Sulayman of Maynila are not treated as automatically identical. The source identifications remain contested.
