The leader of the Macabebe contingent who died fighting the Spanish at Bangkusay on 3 June 1571 is unnamed in the contemporary accounts used for this event. The [National Historical Commission of the Philippines' 2016 Macabebe marker](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ang_Kabataang_Pinuno_ng_Macabebe_NHCP_Historical_Marker.png) commemorates him as **Ang Kabataang Pinuno ng Macabebe** (the young leader of Macabebe), without assigning a personal name. The [NHCP's 2021 Bangkusay marker](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/memorare-bangkusay/) also describes the leader without naming him.

## Names and identification

**Bambalito**, **Tarik Sulayman**, and **Tarik Soliman** are names attributed in later traditions and histories; listing them as searchable aliases here does **not** establish that they were the man's historical name or that all labels originally referred to one person. The name Tarik Sulayman was rejected as undocumented by NHCP researchers when the 2016 marker was prepared. See the [contemporary report of the NHCP decision](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/790311/nhcp-corrects-error-over-true-hero-of-battle-of-bangkusay).

The young Macabebe leader must also be distinguished from **Rajah Sulayman of Maynila**, whose identification with the man killed at Bangkusay is unsupported. This stable page ID retains the older label `bambalito` to preserve links; it is not a claim of proven identity. The reported battle and death are historical; the personal name remains uncertain.
