Tuma steers Bakar's loaded cargo boat in Book II, Chapter 4, *The Empty Mooring*. He explains that a channel navigable by a smaller vessel is not automatically safe for Hamzah's laden boat or wide enough for it to turn. When Ache alters Jamil's passage instruction, Tuma keeps Bakar's vessel on the agreed outer course.

During the encounter with unidentified craft, Tuma maintains room to turn in the wider water and helps bring the carrier Iman aboard from the bank. He later distinguishes his own qualified statement about water depth from any permission for Hamzah to enter the inner cut.

**Continuity limits:** Tuma's judgments are fictional seamanship choices, not documented historical Bruneian techniques. His later voyages and loyalties remain unestablished.
