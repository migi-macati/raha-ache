Veteran captain from the expanded Book I reconstruction.

His working function is to train Ache in river and naval tactics and provide an older military presence around the young heir.

An earlier version had Bulkan accompany Ache and Pasay during the flight south from Maynila. Because Sulad later replaced Pasay as Ache's principal companion, Bulkan's exact place in the current flight sequence remains to be reconciled.

## Flaws and human behavior (fictional)

He prevents mistakes through strict discipline but can reject a safer new method when it comes from a young sailor. He condemns disobedience despite once breaking an order to protect a crewman. He checks an apprentice's food without witnesses and bristles at praise. His role in Ache's escape remains undecided.
