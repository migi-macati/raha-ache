Fictional Bruneian princess and love interest introduced in Part II development.

A distant-cousin relationship to Ache was proposed.

She challenges Ache's belief in personal destiny and pushes him toward institutions, education, and mercy.

She is not automatically identical with Ache's historically attested Bruneian cousin/wife. Whether the fictional Laila becomes that wife remains unresolved.
