Nephew and younger co-ruler or heir of Ache in late-sixteenth-century Maynila.

Riquel records "Raja Ache the Old and Raja Soliman the Young" as lords and principals of Manila.

## Uncertainty

The archive does not equate Sulayman automatically with every figure called Tarik Sulayman or with Bambalito. The Bangkusay identification and the 1572 succession evidence need reconciliation.
