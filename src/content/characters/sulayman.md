Sulayman is the younger ruler associated with Ache in late-sixteenth-century Maynila.

Riquel directly records **Raja Ache the Old** and **Raja Soliman the Young** as lords and principales of Manila.

## Kinship

The project follows the standard reconstruction that Sulayman was Ache's nephew and heir-generation kinsman. The current primary-source notes in the archive, however, do not yet contain a passage that names Sulayman as the same **legitimate nephew** who inherited when Ache died in 1572.

The story bible therefore keeps two propositions separate:

- Sulayman as Ache's younger co-ruler and kinsman.
- The unnamed legitimate nephew in the 1572 succession account.

Their identification may ultimately be correct, but it should be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## Bangkusay

The archive does not automatically equate Sulayman with every figure later called Tarik Sulayman or with Bambalito. The Bangkusay identity problem remains open.
