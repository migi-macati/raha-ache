Nasir is a fictional young worker aboard Hamzah's cargo boat in Book II, Chapter 4, *The Empty Mooring*. He checks the baskets and the terms agreed with the carrying men, including whether buyers will pay for waiting. He owes his sister a share of rice from a sale made the previous week. She expects him back but Hamzah has not guaranteed a return hour.

Nasir questions Ache's suggestion that Hamzah take the inner cut because Safi's lookout boat will be unable to watch the hidden reach. During an encounter with two unidentified craft, Hamzah last sees him near the stern and loses sight of him as men board. The surviving crew return without him. His sister collects the rice already owed and asks to receive direct news of any search.

**Continuity limits:** Nasir is missing, **not confirmed dead or captive**. No witness has established whether he entered the water, reached shore, remained aboard or was taken away. Do not resolve this uncertainty retrospectively without a narrated discovery. Nasir and the incident are fictional.
