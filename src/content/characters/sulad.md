Ache's principal companion and protector in the most recent Part I material.

About sixteen to seventeen years old, like Ache. Darker and more powerfully built than Ache, tattooed or otherwise visibly marked, practical, conservative, and oriented toward survival and combat. He carries a spear and kampilan in the June 2026 visual conception.

Sulad accompanies Ache during the secret flight from Maynila toward Brunei.
