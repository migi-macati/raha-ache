Ache's principal companion and protector in the current Part I material.

About sixteen to seventeen years old, like Ache. Darker and more powerfully built than Ache, tattooed or otherwise visibly marked, practical, conservative, frank, and oriented toward survival and combat.

Sulad is entrusted with Ache's safety and develops a brotherly relationship with him during the flight from Maynila toward Brunei.

The June 2026 visual conception gives him a spear and kampilan.
