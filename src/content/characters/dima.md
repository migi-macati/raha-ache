Dima is a fictional speaker from a household associated with Laoe in Book II, Chapter 6, *The Other King's Shore*. He brings a broken landing post to the Brunei court and disputes the claim that every household of Laoe recognizes the same outside ruler. He agrees to show an armed embassy the outer approach but refuses to guide fighters to his mother's door.

He leaves the expedition before its subsequent assault. **His mother's household's fate is unverified within the narrative.** Dima does not speak for all of Laoe, and his household, testimony and role as guide are invented. Pigafetta's later report of Laoe's sack supplies no names of local witnesses.
