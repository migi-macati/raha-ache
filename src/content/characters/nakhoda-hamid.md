A fictional Bruneian trading captain and longtime courier to Ache's maternal family. In Book I Chapter 3, Hamid's detained vessel forces Ache to test his new understanding of river tallies, authority, and reciprocal obligation. Hamid recognizes a cloth token carried earlier to Ache's mother from Siripada, and offers to teach Ache trading language and ship accounts.

Hamid is an invented character, not a documented historical officer, eyewitness, or messenger. His cargo dispute and the cloth token are novel canon. His direct speech should be practical, teasing, and unwilling to treat royal ancestry as a substitute for competence.

**Character state after Chapter 3:** He recognizes Ache's potential, but remains skeptical of inherited rank. The possibility of later contact through Brunei is planted, not guaranteed.
