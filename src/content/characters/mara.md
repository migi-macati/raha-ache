Mara is a fictional boat owner who accepts Ache as a worker after he helps repair her damaged vessel in Book II, Chapter 6, *The Other King's Shore*. When he moves a hull support without instruction, she pays for the damaged timber from her crew's rice and deducts its price from his earnings. She agrees to carry him only to the next landing, provided he pays for his own food and follows her crew's decisions.

Her agreement is **one leg of a longer return**, not an appointment to Brunei's service or a guarantee of onward passage. She retains control of her boat and her crew. No historical source attests Mara, her vessel, or the specific repair and bargaining events.
