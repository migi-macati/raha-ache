Saliha controls the work of carriers at a fictional eastern landing in Book II, Chapter 5. She has obligations to her brother, whose earlier cargo voyage ended in a disputed loss. A knotted cord preserves a promise she says Yusuf's representative made about replacement cloth.

She refuses to lend her workers to an armed dispute, obtains pay for carrying, negotiates limited storage with an older woman who owns the space, and retains the right to correct Ache's written account. She makes the last mark on the chapter's shore copy, recording labor still owed. The settlement leaves her brother's claim unresolved.
