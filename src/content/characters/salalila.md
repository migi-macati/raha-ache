Salalila is the name used in later historical reconstruction for Ache's father and predecessor in the Maynila ruling line.

## What is directly attested

Aganduru Moriz says Ache's father died when Ache was newly born. The father is not named in that passage.

## Reconstruction

The project follows secondary scholarship that identifies this unnamed father with Salalila, but the name and the father-son relationship are kept as separate propositions.

The story bible therefore treats **Ache had a deceased father** as stronger evidence than **that father was Salalila**.
