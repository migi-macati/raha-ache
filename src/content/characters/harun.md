Harun owns the deeper trading vessel that carries Ache's small northbound party from Brunei in Book II, Chapter 10, *Where the River Lets Go*. He chooses his own buyers, cargo, steersman and crew. He will consider a passage toward Luzon but refuses to promise entry into Maynila's river or participation in a political fight.

Harun negotiates distinct terms with Jamil for limited trade goods, accepts Sulad as a paid working hand and Isan as an interpreter under their own conditions, and keeps the right to turn back if the coast is unsafe. His insistence on sound water jars delays departure and makes Ache bear a real cost.

**Continuity limits:** Harun is a fictional merchant and boat owner. His contracts, route and exact cargo are narrative inventions, not documentary evidence of Brunei maritime practice in 1521. His boat remains under his authority after departure.
