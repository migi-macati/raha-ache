Nima is a fictional member of Mulaw's visiting boat crew in Book I, Chapter 7, *The Length of a Rope*. He is carrying folded cloth belonging to his absent sister. Its covering was already torn when the boat departed, and he says he warned the others before loading. He refuses to let the cloth be set on ground whose owner has not agreed to receive it.

His priority is delivering his sister's property to the buyer she named. He challenges Mulaw's account of the damaged covering and refuses Ache's proposed unloading place, even after Ache pays another man to steady the boat. The later agreement keeps the bundle aboard while the crew repairs its covering and briefly uses the mooring post.

**Continuity limits:** Nima's sister is unnamed and does not appear in the scene. Neither the boat's ultimate destination nor Nima's later relationship with Ache is established. His concern for the cloth is an independent obligation, not proof that he owes Ache a favor or accepts Ache's authority. The character and incident are fictional, not documented historical persons or events.
