Unnamed Bruneian royal woman whom Aganduru Moriz says Ache married after recovering his position in Maynila.

Aganduru describes her as Siripada's granddaughter and Ache's first cousin. The marriage had already been arranged while Ache was in Brunei.

## Novel development

Putri Laila Nur is a separate working fictional love interest. The project has not decided that Laila is the historical cousin/wife.

## Human contradictions (novel development)

**The historically attested woman remains unnamed and privately undocumented.** In fiction she should not exist as a reward for Ache's return. She may want her own Bruneian kin and household protected and sometimes judge Maynila customs unfairly; she must have a specific skill, disliked chore, intimacy, and political disagreement. Her relationship to the separate fictional Laila Nur remains undecided. All emotions here are invented.
