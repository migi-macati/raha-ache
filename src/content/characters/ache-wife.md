Unnamed Bruneian royal woman whom Aganduru Moriz says Ache married after recovering his position in Maynila.

Aganduru describes her as Siripada's granddaughter and Ache's first cousin. The marriage had already been arranged while Ache was in Brunei.

## Novel development

Putri Laila Nur is a separate working fictional love interest. The project has not decided that Laila is the historical cousin/wife.
