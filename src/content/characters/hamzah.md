Hamzah is a fictional Bruneian trader and cargo-boat owner in Book II, Chapter 4, *The Empty Mooring*. He carries rice, cloth and resin, some belonging to other merchants. He insists that the conditions of unloading, waiting and return be distinguished from a convenient expected arrival time. Nasir counts the outward baskets and challenges Hamzah over the buyers' unpaid waiting terms.

When Ache implies that Jamil has authorized a shorter inner passage, Hamzah chooses to take it. Unidentified men intercept the boat in the hidden reach, take goods and tow the hull away. Hamzah returns injured with the surviving crew but cannot account for Nasir's fate. His own loss and the claims of the other merchants remain unsettled.

**Continuity limits:** Hamzah did not receive an actual revised order from Jamil. He is neither absolved of his own choice nor established as responsible for the attackers. His later compensation, the boat's recovery and Nasir's fate are unresolved. Hamzah and the encounter are fictional.
