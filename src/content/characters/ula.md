Babaylan figure from the expanded Book I reconstruction.

Ula connects the Maynila/Namayan world to older ritual and oral traditions around the Pasig and provides a counterpoint to Ache's developing Islamic formation.

The character was first developed around the older Ache–Pasay version. Ula enters the current Ache–Sulad continuity in Book I Chapter 3. She assists a fisher household and gives independent evidence about who cleared the river crossing. Ache initially misreads her as an outsider to formal politics, but her knowledge proves essential to settling a cargo dispute without force. Her small red-thread custom is fictional narrative texture, not an asserted historically documented rite. Ula has her own obligations and is not Ache's religious instructor or subordinate.
