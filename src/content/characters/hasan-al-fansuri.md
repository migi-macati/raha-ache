Sufi scholar from the Fansur/Barus world in the Part II development framework.

Teaches Malay and Arabic and widens Ache's intellectual frame beyond pride, vengeance, and dynastic politics.

## Flaws and human behavior (fictional)

Hasan values correct interpretation and sincere faith, yet can get absorbed in a doctrinal distinction while a person needs practical help. He counsels humility while enjoying the respect given to his learning. He can misunderstand a local custom, own the embarrassment, and refuse to lend his expertise to a dishonest royal pledge.
