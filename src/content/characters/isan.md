Isan is a young interpreter with experience on trading boats that meet Luzon merchants. In Book II, Chapter 10, he accepts a paid place aboard Harun's northbound vessel after discussing the terms with its owner independently of Ache.

Isan can help Ache speak with coastal traders, but he does not claim to know who currently holds Maynila, guarantee passage upriver, or pledge to fight. He requires an agreed return place and refuses entry into a hostile river.

**Continuity limits:** Isan and his employment are fictional. His specific languages, family and later fate are not established; do not expand his linguistic ability or allegiance without subsequent narrative evidence.
