Tamin owns a smaller vessel trading water, salt and other goods between Brunei and outer settlements. In Book II, Chapter 10, he agrees to travel alongside Harun only as far as his own buyers and the first agreed stopping place. He checks his crew's water before accepting the arrangement.

Tamin refuses to surrender provisions already promised to his men and insists that the ruler's letter cannot turn his trading boat into a war vessel. His participation offers limited first-leg support, not a pledged escort to Maynila.

**Continuity limits:** Tamin is fictional. His destination, crew arrangements and goods are invented for the novel. He has not promised to sail to Luzon or fight for Ache.
