Daran is a fictional cargo carrier at the Brunei receiving house. In Book I, Chapter 10, Ache wrongly accuses him of losing a bundle. The bundle was placed where the supervisor had directed. Ache retracts the accusation, and Daran tells him where to stand before the next load. Their relationship begins with an injury to Daran's standing and a limited practical correction, not immediate friendship.

In Book II, Chapter 1, Daran helps maintain the receiving cord and move a merchant's disputed basket of ceramics when water threatens the platform. He knows the work, asks Ache to distinguish what he actually witnessed, and has responsibilities independent of the visiting grandson's hopes. His willingness to work beside Ache does not establish patronage, forgiveness of every slight, or an official position.

The character, dispute and receiving-house procedures are fictional; Daran is not a documented historical person.
