Samad owns the merchant vessel that carries Ache east from Burné in Book II, Chapter 5, *The Price of Passage*. He accepts Ache as a recorder only after defining his food, work and obedience obligations. His crew, including Tarin, retains control of navigation and safety.

Samad requires payment for an imposed delay, refuses to spend his crew's provisions on Ache's voluntary sacrifice, and carries a separately witnessed account back toward Hasan in Burné. He does not promise when the report will arrive or provide Ache with a return berth. His route and voyage are fictional.
