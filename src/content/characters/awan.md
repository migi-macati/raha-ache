Awan is a fictional merchant petitioner in Book II, Chapter 6, *The Other King's Shore*. He seeks restitution for goods withheld at Laoe and presses Brunei's court to respond with force. His receiver identifies some recovered cloth, while other claims remain disputed. He treats the sack as a potential answer to a political and commercial quarrel, though the settlement does not resolve every household's allegiance or liability.

Awan, his shipments and his arguments are invented narrative devices. No surviving near-period account identifies a merchant petitioner by this name in the reported Laoe campaign.
