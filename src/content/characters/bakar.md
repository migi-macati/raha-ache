Bakar owns the trailing cargo boat in Book II, Chapter 4, *The Empty Mooring*. He asks detailed questions about delivery and damage, and insists that his steersman Tuma retain room to work. He warns Ache that Jamil's instruction does not permit a separate inner passage for Hamzah.

When unidentified craft appear, Bakar refuses to take his jars into the hidden reach. His boat and Tuma help retrieve Iman, one of Hamzah's carriers, from the bank. Bakar later gives Jamil an exact account of what Ache said and what Safi's raised hand did not establish. His own buyers must wait after the interrupted voyage.

**Continuity limits:** Bakar's refusal protects an independently owned vessel and its workers; it is not evidence of cowardice or allegiance to the attackers. His later employment and any decision to support a search remain unestablished. Bakar and the encounter are fictional.
