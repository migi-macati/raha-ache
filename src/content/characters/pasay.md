## Historical record

Fray Félix de Huerta describes Pasay as the illegitimate son of Lakan Tagkan of Namayan and a slave woman of Bornean lineage. Huerta associates him with the settlement later known by his name.

## Novel development

An earlier novel outline recast Pasay as Ache's same-age companion during the flight to Brunei. That companion role is superseded by Sulad in the June 2026 Part I material.

Pasay has not been removed from the novel project. His current fictional role is unresolved. A later Part III concept associated him with a southern-shore land grant, but that strand now needs reconciliation with the Sulad-centered version.

## Uncertainty

The possible Pasay–Pasai name connection remains a hypothesis, not an established etymology.
