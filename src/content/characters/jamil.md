Bruneian admiral-mentor introduced in the Part II development framework.

Trains Ache in naval tactics and large-fleet command. Becomes an advocate for Ache within the Brunei court.

## Flaws and human behavior (fictional)

His expertise protects crew lives, but he can protect a favored veteran unfairly and resent innovations from Ache. He is genuinely capable and sometimes tactically wrong. A battle loss should harm his professional pride and another person's livelihood; accountability is not a clever speech. His quiet affection appears in practical checks on crew welfare.
