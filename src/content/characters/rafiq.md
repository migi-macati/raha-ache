Rafiq arrives with two armed kinsmen at the fictional eastern landing in Book II, Chapter 5. He challenges an accusation against his uncle concerning cloth missing from an earlier voyage and demands the carrying share that Karim has withheld.

His escort's weapons create tension, but Rafiq does not automatically authorize violence. He accepts a partial payment for his uncle after a witness confirms the uncle's work, while expressly preserving the dispute about the missing goods. The novel does not establish that Rafiq's uncle stole anything.
