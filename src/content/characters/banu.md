Banu is a fictional boat owner who agrees to carry guards and supplies during the Laoe expedition in Book II, Chapter 6, *The Other King's Shore*. He retains control of his vessel, refuses to enter unknown shallows on another man's assumption, and lends a rope to help free a stranded boat. During the later assault, he takes guards toward shore only after receiving Jamil's order through Ache.

His agreement to carry an expedition does not establish that Brunei's ruler owned his boat. His name, vessel and actions are fictional.
