Non-Muslim Visayan ally in the expanded trilogy framework.

First conceived as a young chief or warrior met during Ache's flight through the Mindoro/Palawan corridor.

In Part II he becomes a guest or ally at Brunei and a bridge between Visayan and Luzon interests.

## Human contradictions (fictional characterization)

He defends local independence and seafaring skill, yet mocks foreign court customs while resenting outsider stereotypes. He enjoys teasing and hospitality, not just war. He can save another crew while refusing to become that captain's subordinate or political ally.
