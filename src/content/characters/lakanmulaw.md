Mulaw is a fictional leader of a visiting boat crew encountered by Ache and Sulad at an unnamed first landing in Book I, Chapter 7. He is responsible to the people traveling with him, including Nima, who is protecting cloth belonging to his absent sister. Mulaw helps negotiate temporary mooring but does not offer Ache passage or promise to return.

His name, position, and encounter are invented for the novel. His influence over the crew is limited by their own interests and decisions. His potential future role as a Visayan ally in Book II and at Brunei remains a development possibility, not a commitment established by this first meeting.

## Development continuity

Earlier outlines called him **Datu Lakanmulaw** and placed his first meeting with Ache in a conjectural Mindoro–Palawan corridor. That is an earlier working conception, not a historically attested name, title, meeting, or route. The current Chapter 7 prose uses the personal name **Mulaw**, assigns no datu or lakan title, and leaves the landing unnamed. The old names remain as aliases to preserve references while later chapters are reconciled.
