Makassarese pilot enslaved in Ache's service according to Aganduru Moriz.

After Ache's release in 1521, Aganduru says Ache gave the expedition this pilot and freed him so he could guide the ships toward the Moluccas.

Other project notes preserve a tradition that Pazeculan understood Castilian; the exact wording and source chain remain to be checked.
