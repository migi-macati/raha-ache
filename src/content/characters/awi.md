Awi is a **fictional** household storekeeper in the final bonus chapter of *Raha Ache*. She is not a person identified in the surviving 1570–1572 Spanish relations.

Awi manages rooms, provisions, repair arrangements and the people who depend on them. Her work rests on negotiated obligations, not an inherited office. She challenges Ache when he offers space already occupied by others and when his promises leave workers to pay the cost.

In [Book III, Bonus Chapter 12](/raha-ache/chapters/book-3-chapter-12/), Awi makes her own arrangements for shelter and care during Ache's illness. The narrative transfers to her first-person testimony only after his death. She witnesses his gift of a copper bowl and refuses to let an unnamed claim to inheritance erase her own ownership.

Her dialogue, relationships, possessions and testimony are inventions of the novel. The historical account's unnamed nephew is not identified through Awi's fictional narrative.
