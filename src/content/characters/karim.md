Karim is a receiving-house figure at the fictional eastern landing in Book II, Chapter 5. He says two rolls from an earlier voyage were missing when the cargo was received and disputes liability for replacement cloth.

He receives five of Yusuf's six new rolls under a provisional arrangement, releases part of an earlier carrying share, and permits the sixth roll to remain under watch pending further testimony. He resents being made to acknowledge these limits before workers and kin. Neither his account nor that of his opponents is established as the complete truth.
