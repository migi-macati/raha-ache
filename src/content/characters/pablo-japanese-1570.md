## Documented record

The Spanish *Relation of the Voyage to Luzon* (1570) reports **twenty Japanese living in Maynila**, and says one man approached the Spaniards, wore headgear the author associated with Christianity, **identified himself as a Christian**, and gave his name as **Pablo** (Paul). It adds that **some people said he was among Maynila's artillerymen**; that final claim is **hearsay**, not established fact.

**Sources:** [1570 primary account, vol. III, p. 82](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/13616/pg13616-images.html); [Ubaldo Iaccarino, Kyūshū–Luzon trade](https://www.ostasien-verlag.de/zeitschriften/crossroads/cr/pdf/CR_10_2013_155-194_Iaccarino.pdf).
