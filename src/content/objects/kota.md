The working Book III concept for Ache’s fortified political center in Maynila.

**The Making of the Kota** is the current title of Book III, Chapter 3. The chapter turns fortification into part of Ache’s state-building: defense, authority, storage, command, and the visible permanence of his return.

The exact form, materials, terminology, and historical model remain to be sourced before the kota is treated as historically reconstructed rather than novel development.
