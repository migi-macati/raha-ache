A surviving complex **warp tie-dyed textile**, excavated in a burial cave context on **Banton Island, Romblon**, in a group of materials dated around the **thirteenth to early fourteenth century**. It demonstrates the age and technical sophistication of Philippine weaving and dying traditions, **but does not document clothing in Ache's Maynila in 1500**.

Novel relevance is **comparative technique**, not direct costume reference. More local evidence is needed before assigning its exact motif, colors, textile origin or use to a Tagalog court.

Source: [National Museum, *Hibla ng Lahing Filipino: The Artistry of Philippine Textiles*](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Hibla-ng-Lahing-Filipino-The-Artistry-of-Philippine-Textiles.pdf).
