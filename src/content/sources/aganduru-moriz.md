## Ache material

Aganduru Moriz gives the project's fullest narrative of Ache's youth and 1521 return:

- father dies when Ache is newly born;
- mother governs Maynila;
- cousin ruling Tondo encroaches on Maynila;
- Ache goes to Brunei for help from his grandfather Siripada;
- nearly two hundred vessels and about six thousand Bruneian soldiers are placed under Ache;
- Ache is captured in the encounter with the European expedition;
- he is released and gives Pazeculan as pilot;
- he returns to Maynila and recovers his lands;
- Tondo is pushed back and peace follows;
- Ache marries a first cousin sent by Siripada.

## Source note

This is a later Spanish chronicle, not a contemporaneous 1521 diary. It preserves details absent from Pigafetta and must be compared against the contemporary expedition accounts rather than treated as automatically controlling.
