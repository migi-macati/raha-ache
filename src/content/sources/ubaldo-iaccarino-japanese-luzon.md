Iaccarino reconstructs trade routes between Kyūshū and Luzon using archival materials and later records. Especially important: **at the time of Martín de Goiti's arrival in Maynila in May 1570, he reports Japanese merchants already there, among them a Christian merchant called Pablo (Paulo)**, referencing Archivo General de Indias, Patronato 24, r. 17.

Japanese links to **northern and western Luzon** are better documented than a specific Japanese colony in Maynila during Ache's youth. Strong evidence for large Japanese settlement and the red-seal ship era belongs to **later decades** and must not be backdated.
