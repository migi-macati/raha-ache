Ryukyu operated an important entrepôt network between East and Southeast Asia during the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, with a wider regional route network including Luzon in scholarly reconstruction. **A transshipment route does not prove a permanent Ryukyuan merchant colony in Maynila.** Scott's survey cautions that surviving Ryukyuan state correspondence lacks direct Philippine entries; weigh this archival gap against more general route arguments.
