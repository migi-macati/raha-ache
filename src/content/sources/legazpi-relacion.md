## Luzon traders

Project notes preserve a report of seven or eight Moros from Luzon at Cebu, led by Magomat, discussing trade and the arrival of Luzon junks.

An experienced Moro pilot reported two Luzon junks in Butuan trading gold, wax, and slaves.

## King of Luzon

The project preserves translated wording in which Legazpi sent word to the king of Luzon expressing a desire to meet him.

The primary passage does not name Ache in that sentence. Identifying the king with Ache is historical reconstruction based on the wider source chain.
