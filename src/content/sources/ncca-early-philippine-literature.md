The NCCA essay describes oral song, riddle, proverb, chant, and poetry, noting **Tagalog song genres** transmitted and named in later accounts such as Chirino and Colin. It is a **secondary synthesis** and may use terms and reconstructed genre lists dating from multiple periods. It is **not a recording of the melodies or lyrics used by Ache** around 1500.

In the novel, use songs, work calls, rhetorical display, riddles, genealogies and storytelling as ways communities remember, negotiate, dispute, grieve and signal belonging. Do not quote later documented songs as if demonstrably composed by Ache's contemporaries.
