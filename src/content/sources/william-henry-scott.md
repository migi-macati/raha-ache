Secondary synthesis used throughout the project for sixteenth-century Philippine society and the political world of Maynila and Tondo.

Project notes drawn from Scott include Ache/Matanda, Lakandula, Sulayman, Siripada/Bulkeiah, Pazeculan, Manila trade, and the title "King of Luzon."

For the separately source-checked 1982 article on plank-built boats, warfare, regional trade and passage vocabulary, see [*Boat-Building and Seamanship in Classic Philippine Society*](/raha-ache/sources/scott-boat-building-seamanship-1982/). Its Butuan and Visayan evidence must not be treated as documentation of Ache's individual Maynila voyage.
