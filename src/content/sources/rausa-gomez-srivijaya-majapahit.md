A foundational critical examination of claims that Srivijaya or Majapahit ruled Philippine territories. Important distinction for the story bible: **Indic influence, Javanese objects, literary place-name claims and actual territorial conquest are different categories of evidence**.

The cited article's bibliographic record is verified; **its complete text was not read in this pass**. The broader assessment that no firm epigraphic evidence establishes Srivijayan or Majapahit rule is reiterated in the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas's [*Ginto* volume](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Media_And_Research/Publications/Ginto.pdf).
