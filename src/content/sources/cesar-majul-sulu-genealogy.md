Explains the uses and limitations of **tarsilas** (genealogical traditions) when reconstructing Islamization and regional dynastic networks. Malay court language reflects participation in wider trading networks but does not establish that every ruler of Sulu shared one ancestry or that Arab/Persian merchants personally visited each Philippine port.
