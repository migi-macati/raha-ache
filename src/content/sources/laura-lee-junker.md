Junker's synthesis of archaeological and historical evidence describes flexible chiefdoms, social ranking, cyclical political aggregation, trade, feasting and military competition. It directly complicates the equation of small, personalized polities with an incapacity for political complexity.

For Ache: model authority as contingent on personal followings, patronage, alliances, accessible surplus and prestige, not necessarily fixed territorial bureaucracy. Different communities have agency and can resist both Ache and Brunei.
