Surveys Philippine actors in Chinese diplomatic and trade records, with particular attention to **Luzon, Pangasinan, Mindoro or Marinduque, and Sulu**. The records of early fifteenth-century missions demonstrate long-distance diplomacy but **do not prove imperial administration of these places**.

A digitized transcription accessible through the [Internet Archive](https://ia800104.us.archive.org/31/items/FilipinosInChinaBefore1500/Filipinos%20in%20China%20before%201500.pdf) discusses joint tribute missions in 1405 and regional rulers' missions in 1406–1411. Date, Chinese character transliteration, exact place identification and whether a mission was sponsored by a particular Maynila dynasty should be checked against the cited Chinese text.
