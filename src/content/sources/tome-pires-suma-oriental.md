**Contemporary written account**, based largely on commercial intelligence gathered from others, not proof that Pires personally visited Luzon.

In his discussion of the *Luções/Lufoes* and Bornean trade, Pires describes vessels moving between Luzon, Brunei and Malacca; trade in gold, wax, honey, provisions, and imported cloth and beads; and Luzon men participating in commercial communities around Malacca. He also describes merchants of Brunei carrying cloth from India's eastern coast and Bengal and glass beads from Cambay, purchased in Malacca, to gold-trading islands.

He gives generalizations about rulers and customs that should **not** be transferred to Ache's exact Maynila household without independent checking. His portrayal of the *Luções* is informed by hearsay and by a Portuguese perspective after 1511.
