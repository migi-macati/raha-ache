Later Franciscan historical source used for Namayan and Pasay traditions.

Huerta describes Pasay as the illegitimate son of Lakan Tagkan by a slave woman of Bornean lineage and connects him to the settlement later known as Pasay.

Because Huerta wrote centuries after the period, the material is kept distinct from contemporaneous sixteenth-century testimony.
