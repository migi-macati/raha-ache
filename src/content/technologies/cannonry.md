Artillery is part of the project’s established Book III development and of later accounts of pre-conquest Maynila.

The current archive links cannonry most directly to Panday Pira and **Book III, Chapter 4: The Making of the Kota**. It does not yet claim a complete reconstruction of local casting methods, gun types, range, deployment, or supply.

In the novel, Ache’s progression into logistics, artillery, and fleet organization should be shown through decisions, constraints, training, and consequences rather than treated as an unexplained technology jump.
