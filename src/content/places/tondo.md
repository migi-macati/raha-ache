Polity north of the Pasig River and major counterpart to Maynila.

Aganduru Moriz describes Ache's unnamed cousin, ruler of Tondo, as encroaching on Maynila while Ache's mother governed. Later Spanish records directly identify Lakandula as principal of Tondo.

The novel treats Tondo as Ache's closest political rival and kin-based counterweight around the bay. Identifying the ruler of Ache's youth with Lakandula remains historical reconstruction.

## Historical setting: river-mouth power

Tondo lay on the north side of the Pasig mouth, facing Maynila across a navigable and politically consequential waterway. Treat it as a neighboring center with its own elites, obligations, and maritime interests, not a province or subordinate district of Maynila.

The **Laguna Copperplate Inscription**, dated to 900 CE, is evidence for an earlier documentary, debt, and authority network in the wider region. The reading and identification of its place names require care: it cannot establish an unbroken dynasty or prove the institutions of Tondo around 1500. Clavé and Griffiths (2022) situate the inscription within Old Malay and maritime Southeast Asian connections.

Aganduru Moriz's later account portrays Ache's unnamed cousin ruling Tondo and encroaching on territory while Ache's mother governed Maynila. The cousin is **not named** in that passage. Identifying him with the Lakandula encountered by Spaniards decades later is an inference, not a primary-source fact.

### Story implications

Rivalry can take the form of contested river access, dependents, dues, witnesses, marriages, patronage, and alliances. A character's claim that a crossing or village belongs to Tondo is a **claim inside the story**, not proof of historical borders. Tondo's leaders should have intelligible motives and internal factions rather than acting as a single villainous bloc.

### Evidence boundaries

- **Supported:** Tondo as a distinct polity and later seat of Lakandula; Aganduru's report of a cousin's encroachment; much older regional documentary traditions.
- **Inferred:** The identification of Ache's cousin with Lakandula; exact succession and relative power of the two polities during Ache's youth.
- **Invented for the novel:** Named toll clerks, disputed ledgers, specific border incidents, and private deliberations.
