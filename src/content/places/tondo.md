Polity north of the Pasig River and major counterpart to Maynila.

Aganduru Moriz describes Ache's unnamed cousin, ruler of Tondo, as encroaching on Maynila while Ache's mother governed. Later Spanish records directly identify Lakandula as principal of Tondo.

The novel treats Tondo as Ache's closest political rival and kin-based counterweight around the bay. Identifying the ruler of Ache's youth with Lakandula remains historical reconstruction.
