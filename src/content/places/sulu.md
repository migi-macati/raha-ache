Sulu-zone setting in the wider maritime world of the trilogy.

Book II development places part of Ache's campaign and alliance arc around Mindanao and Sulu, including the working character Jalal. The exact Sulu episodes remain to be developed.

## Trading and cultural setting

Sulu belonged to interlinked maritime circuits reaching Brunei, Mindanao, Java, the Moluccas and Chinese markets. Its elite genealogy and Islamization narratives include local rulers and incoming Muslim scholars and traders, but an Arab or Persian name in a later account is **not automatically a verified historical visitor to a particular harbor at a particular date**. Religious and political identities developed through layered Asian contacts; Sulu communities had agency distinct from Bruneian ambitions.

A strong research starting point is [Cesar Adib Majul's study of the Genealogy of Sulu](/raha-ache/sources/cesar-majul-sulu-genealogy/), to be read alongside the [foreign-presence dossier](/raha-ache/research/foreign-contacts-ache-era/). Book II's Sulu episodes should incorporate local traders, pilots, hosts, laborers and political interests and avoid treating the region as an undifferentiated raiding zone.
