Sultanate and royal court central to Ache's maternal kinship, political education, and early military career.

Aganduru Moriz describes Ache arriving at his grandfather Siripada's court, being received as a royal relative, and later departing with a large Bruneian force.

In the novel, Brunei is the principal setting of Ache's transition from displaced prince to trained commander.
