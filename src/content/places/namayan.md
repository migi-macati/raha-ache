Pasig-area polity associated in later historical accounts with Lakan Tagkan and Pasay.

Pasay belongs to Namayan's later historical tradition. Earlier novel development also used Namayan as part of the Ache–Pasay storyline, but Pasay is no longer Ache's principal companion.

Namayan remains available for the current Book I world, especially through the Pasig setting and Ula's working material, but its exact role has not yet been fixed.
