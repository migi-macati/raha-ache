Fortified settlement and trading polity at the mouth of the Pasig River.

The project treats Maynila as a port-centered polity whose influence depended on kinship, tribute, trade, and alliances rather than assuming fixed modern territorial borders.

By 1570 the Spanish encountered Ache and Sulayman as senior and younger rulers of Maynila.
