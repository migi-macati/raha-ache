A current Book III development principle: Ache’s Maynila does not immediately replace older religious practices with a single uniform Islamic settlement.

The project preserves religious plurality and tension as part of institution-building. This is novel development unless and until specific practices, communities, and historical arrangements are separately sourced.

The narrative purpose is to make religion part of lived politics, kinship, ritual, legitimacy, and personal belief rather than a simple label applied to the polity.
