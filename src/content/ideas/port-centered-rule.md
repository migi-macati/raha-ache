The project treats Maynila as a port-centered polity whose effective power depended on relationships and flows rather than modern territorial borders.

Kinship, trade, tribute, alliances, military force, river and bay access, and control of exchange all matter to how rule works in the story world.

This is a framing model for writing and reconstruction, not a claim that sixteenth-century Maynila possessed the fixed administrative territory of a modern state.
