## Part I

Birth and youth in Maynila through the flight to Brunei.

Later revision title: **Heir of the River**.

Tondo actively pressures Maynila. Ache's departure becomes a pursued sea journey through the Mindoro/Palawan corridor rather than a simple transition.

Current companion in the June 2026 material: Sulad.

## Part II

Brunei court and training.

Naval command and campaigns.

Friends, rivals, mentors, and love interest.

1521 encounter with the surviving circumnavigation expedition.

Return voyage toward Luzon.

## Part III

Ache returns to the Manila Bay world, retakes or reasserts Maynila, builds institutions, expands alliances, and shapes the political order before direct Spanish conquest.

Later proposed sequence:

13. The Bay That Forgot Him
14. The Making of the Kota
15. The Two Crowns of the Bay
16. Rewarding Loyalty: The Gift of the Southern Shore
17. Old King, New World on the Horizon

The December 2025 version associated the southern-shore land grant with Pasay. The later switch from Pasay to Sulad as Ache's principal companion leaves Pasay's exact novel role unresolved.

## Bonus

Goiti and Legazpi through Ache's final Spanish-contact years, baptism, succession, and death.

This bonus treatment supersedes the older ten-chapter outline that placed the 1570–1572 Spanish sequence inside Book III.
