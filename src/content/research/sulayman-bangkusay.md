Later narratives often identify the leader killed at Bangkusay with Rajah Sulayman of Maynila.

The project keeps that identification open because the surviving source chain is not uniform and the 1572 succession material refers to a legitimate nephew inheriting after Ache's death.

Bambalito, Tarik Sulayman, and Rajah Sulayman are therefore not merged automatically.
