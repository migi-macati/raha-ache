Huerta's Pasay is a male illegitimate son of Lakan Tagkan and a woman of Bornean lineage.

Later colonial spellings sometimes render the place as Pasai, inviting comparison with Samudera Pasai.

There is no contemporaneous precolonial text in the project stating that the person or settlement was named after the Sumatran polity.

Current classification: hypothesis.
