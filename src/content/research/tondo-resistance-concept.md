Historical basis: Aganduru Moriz describes Ache's cousin in Tondo encroaching on Maynila while Ache's mother governed.

Novel expansion: Tondo becomes the leading political counterweight to a Brunei-linked, Islamizing Maynila and can rally other non-Muslim interests around the fear of Bruneian expansion.

The wider Luzon/Visayan religious coalition is extrapolation for the novel, not an established historical alliance.

The Tondo rival should remain a competent political actor with a defensible strategic rationale rather than a simple villain.
