## Evidence status

Secondary accounts of a later narrative attributed to Aganduru Móriz describe a marriage arranged in Brunei and a bride reportedly sent after Ache recovered a position in Maynila. The project's cited original Spanish passage (CODOIN volume 78, printed pages 59–60) has **not yet been collated**. This summary must not be presented as a verified transcription of Aganduru's words.

Some secondary summaries associate the marriage with 1521, but the exact wedding date, location, sequence and kinship have not been established by direct source examination here. The event page's `dateSort` is only a provisional chronology key, not evidence of a specific date.

## Novel continuity

The published Book III Chapter 2 ends with a bounded local restoration and **does not depict a marriage**. Its earlier link to the marriage event was removed. Any later marriage in the novel requires explicit narrative development and a separate distinction between reported history and invented personal scenes.
