## Current

Ache and Sulad are about sixteen to seventeen during the flight to Brunei.

Ache is the Manila heir-prince and skilled young sailor. Sulad is the stronger, darker, tattooed or marked companion/protector and practical warrior.

## Earlier

An earlier reconstruction used Pasay as Ache's same-age companion.

Another development pass introduced Bulkan, Ula, Lakanmulaw, a prolonged Tondo pursuit, and the Manila Bay–Mindoro–Palawan route.

These elements remain development material unless promoted into the current chapter canon.
