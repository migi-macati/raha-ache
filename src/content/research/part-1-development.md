## Current

Ache and Sulad are about sixteen to seventeen during the flight to Brunei.

Ache is the Manila heir-prince and skilled young sailor. Sulad is the stronger, darker, tattooed or marked companion/protector and practical warrior.

The June 2026 visual conception uses a pre-Hispanic Philippine balangay/parao and a night-to-dawn tropical flight through waters resembling the Palawan karst and mangrove environment.

## Earlier

A December 2025 reconstruction placed Ache at about twelve and used Pasay as his same-age companion. That age and companion assignment are superseded by the June 2026 Ache–Sulad material.

The December development pass also introduced Bulkan, Ula, Lakanmulaw, a prolonged Tondo pursuit, naval losses, survival training, and the Manila Bay–Mindoro–Palawan route.

Those supporting elements remain working material unless separately superseded.

An even earlier route concept used Cavite as an intermediate stop. That stop is superseded but retained in the archive.

## Current Chapter 4 research handoff (9 October 2026)

The [Encroachment evidence and scene dossier](/raha-ache/research/book-1-chapter-4-dossier/) develops the two-clerk and crew-count ending of the published Chapter 3 into a witnessed dispute about whether an escort commitment covers one voyage or two. The household retains agency, Tondo's organizer has material reasons, and the mother's limited remedy consumes real labor. This is **fictional scene architecture** constrained by Plasencia's much later 1589 account, not documentation of a c.1500 Maynila registry or a proven incident involving Ache's cousin. Preserve the separate Brunei flight setup and Sulad's right to refuse.
