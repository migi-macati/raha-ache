## Primary-source chain

Current primary-source pages include Albo, Pigafetta, Mafra, the Legazpi expedition relations, Riquel's 1571 record, and the anonymous 1572 Relación.

Aganduru Moriz, Chirino, Morga, and Gaspar de San Agustín are kept as near-primary or early colonial chronicles rather than contemporaneous eyewitness evidence for every event they describe.

## Secondary scholarship

William Henry Scott, Cesar Adib Majul, Luis Camara Dery, Félix de Huerta, Rizal's annotations, and other later works are kept separate from primary testimony.

## Documentary collections

Blair and Robertson is treated as a reference/documentary collection. Where possible, claims should cite the underlying document rather than the collection as though Blair and Robertson were the historical witness.

## Outstanding citation work

- Attach the exact Spanish text and full documentary citation for the 1572 succession passage.
- Attach exact edition/page references for Aganduru Moriz passages used for Ache's youth, Brunei journey, return, and marriage.
- Attach exact document/date/volume/page references for the Legazpi relations currently accessed through documentary collections.

These are citation-completeness gaps, not invitations to silently downgrade or upgrade historical certainty.
