The 1572 succession account says the deceased ruler had no children by his legitimate wife who could inherit and that his property descended to a legitimate nephew.

This is strong evidence that legitimacy of birth affected succession and property transmission.

The identity of the inheriting nephew must be reconciled with conflicting later narratives about Sulayman's fate.
