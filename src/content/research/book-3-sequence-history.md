This page preserves Book III structures that preceded the current version.

## Earlier 10-chapter scaffold

1. Building My Own Fleet
2. Cannonry (Lantaka)
3. Strategy Meeting
4. Liberation of Palawan and Visayas
5. Conquest of Tondo and Liberation of Manila
6. Governor
7. Neighbors
8. The Nephew
9. Welcome to Manila
10. Legacy and Death

The Spanish arrival and Ache's death were later removed from the ten-chapter core.

## Later 13–17 sequence

13. The Bay That Forgot Him
14. The Making of the Kota
15. The Two Crowns of the Bay
16. Rewarding Loyalty: The Gift of the Southern Shore
17. Old King, New World on the Horizon

This sequence sharpened return, institution-building, Tondo rivalry, delegated authority, and succession but retained inherited numbering.

## First ten-chapter reconciliation

1. The Bay That Forgot Him
2. Taking Back Maynila
3. The Two Crowns of the Bay
4. The Making of the Kota
5. Old Alliances, New Debts
6. The Work of Rule
7. Neighbors
8. The Gift of the Southern Shore
9. The Nephew
10. Old King, New World on the Horizon

This fixed the retaking of Maynila but ended Book III without a separate climactic conflict.

## Current reconciliation

The current core retains Chapters 1–8 and gives the book a final internal crisis:

9. **The Broken Oath**
10. **The Kingdom Holds**

The Spanish sequence then continues as:

11. **Bonus: The Foreigners Return**
12. **Bonus: The Last Raha**

This preserves a complete ten-chapter dramatic arc while still carrying Ache's documented life through 1572.
