This page preserves the chapter structures that preceded the current ten-chapter Book III.

## Earlier 10-chapter scaffold

1. **Building My Own Fleet** — Ache turns borrowed Bruneian strength into a Maynila-centered fleet.
2. **Cannonry (Lantaka)** — artillery, gunfounding, defense, prestige, and trade.
3. **Strategy Meeting** — alliance, expansion, trade, and succession pressures.
4. **Liberation of Palawan and Visayas** — old alliances and debts return to Ache's political project.
5. **Conquest of Tondo and Liberation of Manila** — an earlier, more decisive treatment of Ache's recovery of Maynila and conflict with Tondo.
6. **Governor** — defense, economy, trade, learning, and administration.
7. **Neighbors** — relations with surrounding polities and communities.
8. **The Nephew** — Sulayman and succession.
9. **Welcome to Manila** — the Spanish approach, the 1570 battle, Legazpi's arrival, and Bangkusay.
10. **Legacy and Death** — baptism, succession, death, and aftermath.

The current structure no longer treats the Maynila–Tondo relationship as one simple conquest, and Chapters 9–10 moved into the Spanish-contact bonus treatment.

## Later 13–17 sequence

13. **The Bay That Forgot Him**
14. **The Making of the Kota**
15. **The Two Crowns of the Bay**
16. **Rewarding Loyalty: The Gift of the Southern Shore**
17. **Old King, New World on the Horizon**

This later sequence sharpened the return, institution-building, Tondo rivalry, delegated authority, and succession themes but retained inherited numbering that no longer matched the book.

## Current reconciliation

The current Book III uses ten chapters numbered 1–10. It keeps the strongest material from both earlier versions, removes duplicated beats, and leaves the direct Spanish conquest and Ache's death to the bonus chapter.
