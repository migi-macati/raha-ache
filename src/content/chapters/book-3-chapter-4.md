## Chapter purpose

Turn recovered sovereignty into material capacity: fortifications, fleet, workshops, storage, command, and the first visible institutions that can outlast the restoration campaign.

## Opening state

Ache controls Maynila but much of his strength still rests on campaign organization and Bruneian backing.

## Immediate objective

Create a defensible, supplied, locally rooted center of rule.

## Main plot movement

The kota, permanent fleet elements, cannonry, and logistical systems begin taking shape.

## Subplots

- **Panday Pira/cannonry:** technological capability becomes political capacity.
- **Labor and resources:** building exposes the costs of state-building.
- **Sulad/old companions:** informal wartime relationships begin encountering formal roles.

## Conflict and stakes

Resources spent on fortification cannot be spent elsewhere; centralized stores and command also concentrate power and resentment.

## Themes and subthemes

**Theme:** power becomes material.

**Subthemes:** infrastructure, technology, labor, permanence, defense, visibility of rule.

## Character progression

### Ache

**Begins:** thinks like a commander securing territory.

**Pressure:** must plan for maintenance, supply, labor, and systems that work when no battle is happening.

**Ends:** starts thinking like a builder of institutions.

### Panday Pira

**Function:** should have technical judgment and constraints of his own, preventing cannonry from becoming effortless technological uplift.

### Sulad

**Progression:** can begin confronting the difference between personal access to Ache and formal place inside the new order.

## Turning point

Ache chooses durable infrastructure over a more immediately glorious use of resources.

## Resolution and ending state

Maynila is harder to dislodge, but building has created obligations and resource demands.

## Setup and payoff

Pays off Book II's logistics progression and plants institutions that will be tested in Chapters 9–10.

## Handoff

Chapter 5 forces Ache to call on relationships and debts created during earlier journeys.
