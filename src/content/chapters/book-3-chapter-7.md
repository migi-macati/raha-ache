A stronger Maynila changes the calculations of everyone around it.

- Relations with neighboring Tagalog, Kapampangan, highland, Visayan, and other communities.
- Marriage, kinship, trade, literacy, tribute, force, and reputation become different tools of policy.
- Ache cannot treat every neighbor as either subject or enemy.
- The chapter widens Book III from rebuilding Maynila to shaping a regional order.
