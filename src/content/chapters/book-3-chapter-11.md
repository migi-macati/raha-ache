Years after Ache's kingdom-building crisis, the foreign power foreshadowed by the 1521 encounter reaches Manila Bay in force.

- Earlier reports and messages from the Spanish expedition acquire new meaning.
- Martín de Goiti reaches Maynila in 1570.
- Negotiation collapses into battle and the fortified settlement is overrun and burned.
- The Spaniards withdraw rather than permanently occupying Manila that year.
- Legazpi returns in 1571.
- Ache and Lakandula pursue accommodation while Sulayman's position is more resistant.
- Resistance around the bay culminates at Bangkusay, while the Bambalito/Tarik Sulayman identity problem remains explicitly unresolved in the story bible.

**Bonus function:** show the historical crisis that arrives after Book III's internal dramatic arc has already ended.
