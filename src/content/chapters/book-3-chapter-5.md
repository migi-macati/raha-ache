Ache's earlier journeys and campaigns return as political consequences.

- Allies from Palawan and the Visayas are no longer simply companions from an adventure; they have interests of their own.
- Old favors become requests, obligations, bargaining power, and possible alliances.
- Ache learns that a wider maritime network cannot simply be "liberated" or absorbed by decree.
- The chapter reframes the older **Liberation of Palawan and Visayas** concept around reciprocity, diplomacy, and negotiated power.
