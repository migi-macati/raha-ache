## Chapter purpose

Make governance dramatic by showing that rules solve real problems while redistributing advantage and creating resentment.

## Opening state

Ache has territory, defenses, and alliances but too many decisions still depend on personal access and ad hoc judgment.

## Immediate objective

Create procedures for trade, disputes, records, naval obligations, correspondence, and religious coexistence.

## Main plot movement

The working institutions of Maynila become explicit: harbour administration, naval order, scribal practice, and rules that can operate repeatedly.

## Subplots

- **Sulayman:** receives or resists increasing responsibility.
- **Ache's wife:** can expose domestic and dynastic consequences of public rules.
- **Traders and scribes:** give institutional changes human beneficiaries and losers.
- **Religious settlement:** tests rule across plural communities.

## Conflict and stakes

Every standard reduces someone's discretion. Rules that make trade predictable can also feel like intrusion or extraction.

## Themes and subthemes

**Theme:** order always distributes power.

**Subthemes:** bureaucracy, fairness, taxation, recordkeeping, pluralism, unintended consequences.

## Character progression

### Ache

**Begins:** assumes better rules create better government.

**Pressure:** discovers that even sensible rules create winners, losers, loopholes, and resistance.

**Ends:** becomes more attentive to implementation and legitimacy, not only design.

### Sulayman

**Progression:** moves closer to real governance and begins forming judgments that may differ from Ache's.

### Ache's wife

**Progression:** should emerge as political observer/participant whose Bruneian background gives her a comparative view of Ache's institutions.

## Turning point

A rule Ache considers obviously beneficial creates an unexpected dispute or political cost.

## Resolution and ending state

The institutions remain, but Ache now understands that administration is continuous negotiation.

## Setup and payoff

Directly plants the grievances that later contribute to the coalition crisis.

## Handoff

Chapter 7 shows how neighboring powers react to the stronger, more organized Maynila.
