## Chapter purpose

Test the transition from personal loyalty to formal delegated authority and directly plant the political tensions that will drive Chapters 9–10.

## Opening state

Ache understands that he cannot directly administer every shore or relationship.

## Immediate objective

Place trusted authority on the southern shore while preserving Maynila's wider order.

## Main plot movement

Ache grants land, office, or delegated jurisdiction to a trusted figure. The exact beneficiary remains unresolved between the older Pasay concept and later Sulad development.

## Subplots

- **Ache and Sulad/Pasay:** friendship or loyalty becomes politically unequal once office and territory are involved.
- **Sulayman:** observes how Ache distributes power and may question the precedent.
- **Local interests:** people affected by the grant have their own claims and customs.

## Conflict and stakes

Delegation can extend Ache's order or create a semi-independent power center. Rewarding one loyalist can alienate others.

## Themes and subthemes

**Theme:** trust becomes dangerous when formalized as power.

**Subthemes:** delegation, patronage, autonomy, favoritism, office versus friendship.

## Character progression

### Ache

**Begins:** knows he must share authority.

**Pressure:** discovers that choosing whom to trust is itself a political act with losers.

**Ends:** delegates anyway, accepting that control and trust cannot coexist perfectly.

### Sulad / Pasay

**Progression:** whoever receives the grant must move from personal follower/ally to office-holder with obligations to people beyond Ache.

### Sulayman

**Progression:** begins judging Ache not only as elder ruler but as architect of a succession system he may inherit.

## Turning point

The grant is accepted but triggers visible resentment, fear, or competing claims.

## Resolution and ending state

Ache extends authority, but the coalition of those threatened by his wider reforms now has a concrete grievance around which to organize.

## Setup and payoff

Direct setup for **The Broken Oath**.

## Handoff

Chapter 9 reveals the accumulated political cost of Chapters 4–8.
