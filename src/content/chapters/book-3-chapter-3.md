Ache begins turning recovered power into something that can hold.

- Fortification and the making of the kota.
- Cannonry and gunfounding become part of Maynila's defensive system.
- River and sea approaches are treated as one defensive problem.
- Storage, workshops, command, trade, and government begin to gather around the fortified center.
- The chapter absorbs the useful material from the older separate **Cannonry (Lantaka)** chapter.
