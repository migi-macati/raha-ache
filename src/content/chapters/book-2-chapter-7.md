## Chapter purpose

Introduce the surviving circumnavigation expedition from Ache's perspective and destabilize the confidence produced by Chapter 6.

## Opening state

Ache is returning from success with a strong sense that he now understands maritime power.

## Immediate objective

Identify the unfamiliar ships, assess their intentions, and protect his force while continuing home.

## Main plot movement

Contact with the European ships escalates toward conflict because Ache and the opposing force interpret each other through incomplete information.

## Subplots

- **Curiosity versus caution:** Ache wants to understand what he is seeing.
- **Command reputation:** officers respond to the confidence Ache has recently earned.
- **The wider world:** Brunei and Luzon are placed inside a global maritime system neither Ache nor the reader should treat as isolated.

## Conflict and stakes

Misreading unfamiliar technology, tactics, intentions, or signaling can turn contact into disaster.

## Themes and subthemes

**Theme:** mastery is local.

**Subthemes:** uncertainty, first contact, intelligence failure, technological difference, overconfidence.

## Character progression

### Ache

**Begins:** proven commander at the height of confidence.

**Pressure:** faces data that do not fit his categories.

**Ends:** commits to a course whose risks he cannot fully calculate.

### Elcano / expedition figures

**Function:** should be presented as actors with their own fear, scarcity, goals, and misunderstandings rather than as omniscient representatives of Europe.

## Turning point

Communication or maneuver is interpreted as hostile and the possibility of peaceful disengagement collapses.

## Resolution and ending state

The clash becomes unavoidable.

## Setup and payoff

Pays off Ache's strategic habits by testing their limits. Sets up the documented 1521 capture.

## Handoff

Chapter 8 delivers the reversal.
