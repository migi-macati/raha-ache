## Chapter purpose

Teach Ache to negotiate from weakness and make logistics a visible part of leadership.

## Opening state

Ache has survived through skill and emerging alliances but lacks the material base to continue safely.

## Immediate objective

Secure food, water, passage, boats or repairs, and local protection without the ability to command them.

## Main plot movement

The fugitives obtain what they need to continue toward Brunei.

## Subplots

- **Ache and Sulad:** disagreement over acceptable bargains or risks.
- **Reputation:** Ache begins creating a name outside Maynila through behavior rather than inheritance.
- **Lakanmulaw/alliance network:** prior cooperation may create leverage.

## Conflict and stakes

Every bargain costs something: goods, promises, future obligations, vulnerability, or time that pursuit can exploit.

## Themes and subthemes

**Theme:** authority versus persuasion.

**Subthemes:** reciprocity, logistics, debt, humility, negotiation.

## Character progression

### Ache

**Begins:** still instinctively thinks like someone entitled to resources.

**Pressure:** must ask, bargain, promise, and accept refusal.

**Ends:** understands logistics and diplomacy as forms of power.

### Sulad

**Progression:** becomes increasingly willing to challenge Ache's promises when he thinks Ache is buying survival with dangerous future debts.

## Turning point

Ache secures a necessary resource not by rank or force but by reading the interests of another community.

## Resolution and ending state

The voyage can continue, but it now carries obligations that may matter later.

## Setup and payoff

Plants debts and relationships that Book III can reactivate as **Old Alliances, New Debts**.

## Handoff

With supplies secured, the remaining obstacle is the final pursuit and the sea itself.
