The institutions Ache built begin producing resistance of their own.

- Several captains and local power-holders refuse new dues, naval obligations, appointments, or enforcement.
- Their grievance is not simply hatred of Ache: they believe his reforms are turning negotiated relationships into permanent central authority.
- Trade is disrupted and parts of the bay hesitate to answer Maynila's commands.
- Sulayman wants a decisive response before defiance spreads.
- Ache realizes that personally crushing the coalition would prove he still has only personal power.

The chapter ends with the coalition openly challenging the order Ache has built.
