The homeward expedition reaches the threshold of Luzon.

- Ache is no longer the displaced youth who arrived in Brunei.
- His fleet command, campaign experience, alliances, and survival of the 1521 encounter have completed the apprenticeship begun at Siripada's court.
- The choice is now irreversible: he will confront the political order that took shape while he was away.
- The final image is home ahead, not home already won.

**Book II resolution:** Ache has become capable of attempting the recovery of Maynila.

**Not resolved here:** the retaking of Maynila. That is the opening conflict of Book III.
