Ache leaves Brunei at the head of the force that will carry him back toward Luzon.

- The Brunei arc resolves: he arrived as a refugee and leaves as a commander.
- His authority now rests partly on demonstrated skill, not only descent.
- He has survived failure, campaigning, command, and capture.
- Siripada commits major military backing to the return.
- Ache accepts that the next victory cannot merely restore his old life; he will have to build something that can survive after the Bruneian fleet leaves.

Book II ends at sea with the fleet turning north.

Maynila has **not** yet been retaken. That is the opening external conflict of Book III.
