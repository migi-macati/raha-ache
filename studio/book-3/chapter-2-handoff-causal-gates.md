# Book III Chapter 2 — causal handoff audit

**Packet:** RA-B3C2-HANDOFF-01. **State:** research and architecture proposal, not canon or reader-facing prose. **Scope:** compare the Chapter 2 outline on main with Book II Chapter 10's published ending and the six-scene Book III Chapter 1 Studio draft. **Checkpoint:** Book III Chapter 1 until its public release is verified.

## Contradictions in the current outline

The main Chapter 2 outline presumes Ache begins with armed force, uses a Bruneian expedition, defeats a historically named Lakandula, and introduces a dynastic marriage. The actual published story does not establish these conditions.

- Book II ends with **Harun's private trading vessel**, a limited Bruneian letter and voluntary passage by Sulad and Isan. Harun can leave after trading; no state expedition or army exists.
- Book III Chapter 1 earns **three nights of paid lodging** and a possible hearing. It does not establish a militia, second vessel, alliance, recognized title or wedding. Amaya says Ache's mother instructed workers to keep the old hall productive.
- Dery (1992) hypothesizes a genealogy in which Lakandula could be Ache's adult contemporary, but his exact early-1520s office and participation are unproven. Do not assign him a battle merely from 1571 evidence.
- Laila remains in Brunei by independent choice. She is not established as a wife or fiancée. The identity and date of any historical marriage remain uncertain.
- Aganduru's cited Spanish pages 59–60 remain uninspected. Do not present them as verified support for this restoration.

## Minimum causal requirements

**Legitimacy:** Amaya and independent witnesses must establish what Ache's mother actually entrusted to the working household and what claim Ache can defend. Her present whereabouts remain unknown.

**Resources:** Name the owners of boats, food, weapons if any, labor and landing rights. Show payment, incentives and refusals. Harun's cargo and vessel cannot be appropriated.

**Time:** Three nights of lodging cannot plausibly produce a force capable of retaking a polity. Show weeks or months passing if required, with costs, setbacks and opportunities for the opposition to act.

**Opposition:** Tondo's far-bank interests have their own boats, workers and reasons to resist. A trader's message is not a decree from a named historical ruler.

**Resolution:** Specify what constitutes Maynila's restoration: a defensible principal landing, household jurisdiction, local acknowledgment and navigable access. A tactical success without recognition does not resolve the political conflict.

**Cost:** Ache's strategy must damage a particular supporter or household, leaving a grievance that Chapter 3 must address.

## Provisional six scenes

1. **The Hearing:** Ache presents Amaya and other witnesses. He relinquishes immediate ownership demands and obtains only a bounded claim. The far-bank envoy may challenge his authority.
2. **Those Who Will Carry Him:** Local owners and crews negotiate terms of a limited alliance. Sulad independently chooses whether to join; Harun can depart after trade.
3. **The Months Between:** Explicit passage of time through tides, repairs, wages and shifting loyalties. Earn the boats, food and local supporters on page.
4. **The River Held Narrow:** A rival interest controls access to a landing or channel. Ache's tactical judgment meets an actual political and civilian cost.
5. **The Price of a Door:** Ache secures a decisive position, then forgoes a destructive advantage to preserve people and livelihoods he hopes to govern.
6. **Maynila Open Again:** A concrete restoration of authority over Maynila is accepted by local witnesses and boat owners by chapter end. Tondo retains leverage and a grievance for Chapter 3's settlement.

**Source boundary:** This is a fictional causal plan. It does not establish an exact homecoming date, 1520s Lakandula office, dynastic marriage or a documented conquest. Preserve Book III's ten core chapters and bonus 11–12.

**Release gate:** Re-read published Chapter 1, not merely Studio, before drafting Chapter 2. Confirm ownership, payments, the mother's exact reported instruction, and Banu's limited promise. Require passing exact-head CI and Pages deployment before advancing any chapter checkpoint.
