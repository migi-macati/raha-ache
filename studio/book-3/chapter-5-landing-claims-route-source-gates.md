## Six-scene causal tests, with physical consequences

1. **The Claim Beside the New Step:** On the morning after the trial, Raya returns to inspect the step after high water; Dala brings a damaged cord/frame claim, Amaya asks for Ache's personal cloth, and the keeper distinguishes delivered goods from future offers. A visiting buyer requests priority for a cargo **larger than the single-step test**. Ache nearly offers a future share he cannot own. The keeper refuses to witness it. End with an actual lost or delayed transaction, not an abstract debate.
2. **The Name on the Boat:** Buyer knows a crew member who *might* have heard Mulaw's name. Paying to inquire requires Ache's own labor or property, not the hall's account, Dala's space, or Nima's sister's cloth. A person may carry an inquiry without promising a reply.
3. **Two Accounts of a Favor:** A witness corrects Ache's memory of an earlier favor; the supposed beneficiary and payer differ. A physical object and testimony must complicate the claim. Someone refuses an unauthorized transfer.
4. **The Cargo That Could Leave:** The smaller boat's successful test does not entitle a large merchant to exclusive priority or storage. Keeper, Dala, Amaya, and rival owner expose incompatible terms. A vessel can choose another bank.
5. **What Mulaw Did Not Promise:** Contact may fail or arrive with independent obligations. Mulaw and Nima have genuine choices; no reunion automatically solves the chapter. Ache gives up an attractive demand and makes a narrower reciprocal offer.
6. **The Words They Could Carry:** Witness a time-limited, capacity-limited agreement with actual contributors, conditional repair inspection and dispute path. Leave unpaid claims visible until paid. End on a disputed tally mark or an absent owner's claim that can propel Chapter 6's records/appeals conflict.
