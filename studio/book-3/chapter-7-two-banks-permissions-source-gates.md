## Exact handoff from published Chapter 6

The neighboring woman asks, *“If he does not bring it, who tells him he has waited long enough?”* The keeper looks to Amaya and then Ache; Ache **waits for Amaya to answer**. Open Scene 1 in that same doorway and allow Amaya to answer for herself. Ache owes one cloth of the measure Amaya displayed, fit for household use; no deadline was accepted, and no payment has occurred. Amaya will receive the cloth herself, not through a deposit in the hall. The keeper recorded the obligation without a date. Do not give Ache money, cloth, stores or a payment date he has not earned.

Other separate unresolved claims: Dala's frame needs a suitable wooden brace; the trial binding has not been made or accepted, and lost catch remains unpaid. Raya must inspect the upper step after high water; the lower berth is unfinished and the sounding stake is not a mooring post. Tawan has paid his crewman **half** his waiting compensation and still owes the other half; the steersman has not accepted a payment or agreed to work again. The basket maker had to find another jar carrier after Ache broke his promise. Madan's timber was already paid for. No automatic permission exists across Dala's fishing path. The unnamed woman who paid for the original two-basket journey is distinct from Sera and Lana.

## Evidence and what it cannot establish

**Contemporaneous trade context:** Tomé Pires, *Suma Oriental* (written c.1512–1515), describes Luções and commerce connecting Luzon, Borneo and Malacca, including foodstuffs, wax and honey. A published discussion with translated excerpts appears in *Asian Studies* 15 (1977), https://asj.upd.edu.ph/mediabox/archive/Vol15%2CApr-Aug-Dec1977.pdf (see printed p.24). This establishes plausible maritime trade in the region, **not** a two-bank Maynila compact, boatman's phrase, landing fee, or specific cargo. The original Portuguese wording and textual transmission have not been collated.

**Physical river setting:** The Pasig links Manila Bay and Laguna de Bay; its flow is affected by tides and relative water levels, as described in the 2014 environmental overview https://www.cibtech.org/J-GEOLOGY-EARTH-ENVIRONMENT/PUBLICATIONS/2014/Vol_4_No_3/04-JGEE-004-KARL-EUTROPHICATION.pdf . Modern observations support general estuarine mechanics, **not** an exact early-sixteenth-century shoreline, depth, channel, tidal hour, season or boat design. Do not invent a precise tide table or modern engineered bank. Use observed current, mud, rope, load and waiting as **fictional local conditions**.

**Writing and differentiated obligations:** The 900 CE Laguna Copperplate Inscription (Clavé and Griffiths, 2022, https://archium.ateneo.edu/phstudies/vol70/iss2/2/) prevents a blanket assertion that precolonial Luzon lacked written obligations. It does not prove Ache's bamboo strips or a standardized public docket. Plasencia's 1589 *Customs of the Tagalogs*, https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Philippine_Islands%2C_1493%E2%80%931898/Volume_7/Documents_of_1589 , is later, colonial-context testimony and cannot assign automatic service duties to Chapter 7's workers or shoreline households.

**Political boundary:** Book III Chapter 3 shows bounded, disputed dealings with Tondo; it is not a treaty of submission. No verified early-1520s personal participation by Lakandula, Sulayman or Mehomete; do not introduce an office or genealogical fact merely to satisfy the old Book III summary. No historical attestation for the neighboring woman, her household, the boat operator, the keeper's tally practice or Ache's negotiations. Those are fiction.

## Before each scene: distinct transaction and irreversible turn

1. **Amaya's answer:** At the doorway, Amaya decides what she will do if no cloth is forthcoming, without claiming to set a date she has not agreed. The neighbor observes the keeper's limited record and asks to bring a complaint about her own household's path or goods. Ache offers to *hear* her, not compel anyone outside his household. End with an identified object, injury or impending delivery, not a general speech about justice.
2. **The other bank's boat:** Distinguish merchant, boat owner, boat workers, goods owner, the shore-holder across the river and Dala's separate Maynila path. Introduce the visiting merchant's name in scene; never conflate her with the unnamed Chapter 6 witness. A phrase heard as one landing by one party is heard as both shores by another. Show who actually said it, in what language or words, and who could hear it. No guaranteed professional interpreter.
3. **A physical refusal:** Raya assesses the step's capacity after high water, Dala refuses or bounds use of her net path, and the opposite bank's household can similarly refuse. Let current, load and hands change the bargain; do not replay the tally misunderstanding.
4. **Tondo rumor:** A messenger reports a claim of exclusive fees/protection; Ache checks the exact Chapter 3 limits and corrects the rumor publicly even at a reputational cost. Messenger speaks for one household only, not Lakandula or all Tondo.
5. **Consent on each shore:** One named crossing with separate terms for boat, goods, carrying and shore access; read back the terms to those present, allow refusal, and make the costs fall on a named party. A witness must be able to report only what was heard.
6. **One crossing or accountable cancellation:** Demonstrate one bounded passage if safe, or refuse unsafe passage and show the loss. Revisit Amaya's unresolved claim; end with the practical problem of a witness carrying terms beyond Ache's sight. Chapter 8, not this scene, tests delegated authority.

## Literary and QA requirements

Ache's first-person perception is limited; he must be capable of error and temptation to overclaim. Amaya, Dala, Raya, the neighbor, merchants and workers have reasons to refuse him. Do not write six hearings or an abstract institutional manifesto. Every scene must change a specific permission, route, debt, piece of work or relationship. Material vocabulary should remain tied to what the character can touch, hear, carry or see. No court, standardized toll, appointed governor, sovereign fleet, automatic labor conscription or completed marriage. Retain 30 core chapters and Book III bonus Chapters 11–12. Review *Neighbors* as a working title once the chapter exists.

**Open evidence limits:** original Portuguese Pires, original Spanish Aganduru CODOIN 78 pp.59–60, exact early-1520s Maynila landing customs, rendered-live Pages and actual Read Aloud playback. Treat all invented transactions as fiction.
