# Book III Chapters 9–10: causal-transition experiment (Studio only)

**Status:** editorial hypothesis, 1 November 2026. Published chapters unchanged. Base main `e77e01991f9cbd799d17dd2295b4f25057681f71`. No claim of reader validation.

## Verified textual anchors

- III.8 ends with Ache holding Raya's timber while she marks sound grain. The owner redirects traders to another landing.
- III.9 begins from that exact timber action and ends with Ache taking Amaya's empty basket to fetch jars; the owner still has enough willing traders to go elsewhere.
- III.10 begins with jars touching in Amaya's basket. Its ending shows a passenger landing by the older stones after sounding the water, while Ache accepts the limits of the repaired step and answers a call for carrying work.
- Across these endings, Ache repeatedly learns to accept limited agency through work, consultation and refusal to claim others' authority. The repetition is grounded in the prose, but whether it harms readers is not independently tested.

## Controlled A/B test

**A: published version (control).** Preserve the exact current III.9–10. The trade diversion, jars and practical work are coherent, but the final reversal is understated and resembles preceding endings.

**B: character-led structural proposal, not replacement prose.**

1. In III.9, bring the contested watcher charge and diverted boat into one decision: Ache must publicly choose between claiming authority to retain the trade and admitting he cannot guarantee safe passage or levy an unagreed charge. The merchant's departure must cause a **specific, sustained loss** (work, supplies or trust) to someone already present. Do not manufacture a historical political office or command.
2. Have Amaya, Dala or Raya react from an independently established stake, rather than delivering another correction of Ache's ethics. One relationship should be changed in a way that cannot be reset by a new hearing.
3. In III.10, make Ache's decision address that loss, not merely restate his willingness to carry baskets. His sacrifice should foreclose a personally desired status, privilege or immediate reunion; avoid a convenient resolution of his mother's fate.
4. Keep the old stones, the limited repaired step, Harun's merchant terms, independent landing authorities and the single-passenger ending if the latter remains the strongest image. Preserve the water-level safety distinction.
5. A maternal-thread payoff must be evidence-bounded: a credible limited report or a demonstrably failed search with an emotional consequence. A new message cannot conveniently certify death or reunion.

## Acceptance tests before public prose changes

- Write a six-scene before/after causal map for III.9 and III.10. Every new beat needs an earlier cause and a later consequence; distinguish III.9's irreversible loss from III.10's final choice.
- Compare approximate word counts, scenes devoted to hearings/consent explanations, dialogue voice differentiation and chapter endings. Cut repetition only where no new plot or relationship information is lost.
- Verify the choice cannot be undone by a subsequent bargain; record what Ache loses, who else pays and who can still refuse.
- Compare continuity with III.1 maternal question, III.8 diverted trade, and bonus III.11–12 (including the mother's copper bowl). No new historically asserted event without evidence.
- Seek real reader/editor feedback if available. Keep A intact until B is demonstrably stronger, passes exact-head CI, and receives a scoped publication PR.

**Decision:** Prototype B as a Studio scene map first; no authorization to replace the published ending based on this memo alone.
