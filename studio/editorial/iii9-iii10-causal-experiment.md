# Book III Chapters 9–10: causal-transition experiment (Studio only)

**Status:** scene-indexed editorial hypothesis, 1 November 2026. Published chapters unchanged. Base main `e77e01991f9cbd799d17dd2295b4f25057681f71`. No claim of reader validation.

## Verified textual anchors

- III.8 ends with Ache holding Raya's timber while she marks sound grain. The owner redirects traders to another landing.
- III.9 begins from that exact timber action and ends with Ache taking Amaya's empty basket to fetch jars; the owner still has enough willing traders to go elsewhere.
- III.10 begins with jars touching in Amaya's basket. Its ending shows a passenger landing by the older stones after sounding the water, while Ache accepts the limits of the repaired step and answers a call for carrying work.
- Across these endings, Ache repeatedly learns to accept limited agency through work, consultation and refusal to claim others' authority. The repetition is grounded in the prose, but whether it harms readers is not independently tested.

## Controlled A/B test

**A: published version (control).** Preserve the exact current III.9–10. The trade diversion, jars and practical work are coherent, and both chapters already contain concrete commercial losses. The unresolved question is whether the losses change Ache's final options and relationships enough to earn a trilogy climax.

**B: character-led structural proposal, not replacement prose.**

1. In III.9, bring the contested watcher charge and diverted boat into one decision: Ache must publicly choose between claiming authority to retain the trade and admitting he cannot guarantee safe passage or levy an unagreed charge. **Preserve** the existing fish-sale loss in III.9 scene 11–12, the missed Tesa work and Amaya's unpaid cloth. Test whether the trade diversion and misrepresented mark can cause an additional *change of choice* rather than inventing another loss. Do not manufacture a historical political office or command.
2. Have Amaya, Dala or Raya react from an independently established stake, rather than delivering another correction of Ache's ethics. One relationship should be changed in a way that cannot be reset by a new hearing.
3. In III.10, make Ache's decision address that loss, not merely restate his willingness to carry baskets. His sacrifice should foreclose a personally desired status, privilege or immediate reunion; avoid a convenient resolution of his mother's fate.
4. Keep the old stones, the limited repaired step, Harun's merchant terms, independent landing authorities and the single-passenger ending if the latter remains the strongest image. Preserve the water-level safety distinction.
5. A maternal-thread payoff must be evidence-bounded: a credible limited report or a demonstrably failed search with an emotional consequence. A new message cannot conveniently certify death or reunion.

### Revised diagnosis after scene inventory

**Corrected earlier overstatement:** III.9 and III.10 *already* depict concrete lost fish sales, missed paid work, trade diversion, extra carrier expense, a real engineering failure and the continued cloth debt. It would be false to call the chapters consequence-free. The narrower P1 hypothesis is **dissociation between those losses and Ache's decisive personal transformation**: the commercial losses chiefly fall on Sela and other owners, while Ache ends with a familiar promise to work. Test whether a stronger choice can be earned from these existing costs without manufacturing a new disaster.

**Most defensible compression candidates, not yet prose edits:** III.9 scenes 3–4 (setup/hearing), 6–7 (measure/sourcing), 9–10 (rumor/correction); III.10 scenes 3–4 (separate complaint and labor), 11–12 (step test/ground conditions), 16–17 (two accounting hearings). These are *candidates*, not automatic deletions: each currently contains information and independent agency. A viable revision must preserve witnesses, owner-specific limits, causality and dates.

Carry forward Sela's unpaid loss, Amaya's precise cloth claim, Ache's failed bid for authority and his actual Tesa earnings. State which concrete privilege Ache relinquishes and which person can still refuse. Keep the public manuscript unchanged until the alternative demonstrates a more powerful climax without weakening the existing moral and historical specificity.
