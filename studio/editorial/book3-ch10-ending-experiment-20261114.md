# Book III.10 — ending experiment (unpublished)

**Purpose:** Test a cost-bearing final action against the existing ending. Do not replace published prose without continuity and reader testing. The current ending is the control.

## Alternative final passage

Behind me someone called for a carrier. I looked at the sun before I answered.

Marun stood with a narrow board on his shoulder. A man beside him wanted it taken to the upper bend before dark. He had paid Marun for the shorter way, past Raya's new step.

“Can we cross there?” the man asked.

“Raya tested one carried load. She closed the step when the water rose.”

“It looks sound.”

“It looked sound before she closed it.”

The man studied the water. “The higher path takes another hour.”

I had promised Amaya I would ask Tesa for work tomorrow. I could carry the board now, earn a little grain, and return before dark. The man offered less for the longer journey. He had priced the short one and would not pay for a mistake he had not made.

I thought of the cloth I still owed Amaya. I could say the step would hold. I could tell myself Raya had tested it, and the man would have his board at the bend while there was still light.

“I will take the higher path,” I said. “For what you offered.”

Marun looked at me. “You needn't.”

“I know.”

We lifted the board. At the narrow turn a woman came toward us with water on her head. We lowered the wood to let her pass. She went on without thanking us. We had taken up the whole path.

At the upper bend the man paid the smaller measure. I counted it in my palm. It would buy less cloth than the grain I might have earned by the river, if the step had held.

Marun went home with his wage. I took mine toward Amaya's house. Below us a boat passed the landing without slowing. I did not call it back.

## Acceptance and rejection

**Control:** The published III.10 ends with Ache checking the sun before answering a call for a carrier, a quiet indication that he now respects the obligations of paid work.

**Experiment:** Makes the choice observable and costly. Ache declines to certify Raya's unsafe or untested crossing and accepts less pay toward Amaya's cloth. Marun and the customer retain their own terms.

**Risk:** Another wage-and-passage transaction could intensify the procedural repetition diagnosed in III.7–10. The cost may be too modest for the book's larger political and familial promises.

**Decision gate:** Compare both endings after rereading III.7–10 and I.10/II.10. Seek independent core-only reader responses on emotional force, surprise, Ache's transformation and didacticism. Reject this experiment if the original is stronger. This does not resolve Ache's mother's absence or the larger Book III political contract. No historical event or published canon is changed.
