# Trilogy promise/payoff: the absent mother and the meaning of command

**Studio diagnosis, 14 November 2026.** Grounded in the published endings of I.10, II.10, III.1, III.9–10 and targeted mother/Sulad passages in III.2 and the two bonuses. This is a bounded editorial argument, not an assertion that all intervening scenes have been rechecked. No published prose or canon changed.

## The promise made

**I.10:** Ache dictates a message to his mother from Brunei: he is alive, has shelter, and no force has been promised. The witnesses have no carrier, so the message has **not been sent**. He initially speaks for Sulad without consent, then corrects himself. Sulad explicitly wants his own message sent. Ache stays and learns to mark only bundles actually received. This establishes two distinct promises: news must reach his mother; Sulad must remain a person who can answer for himself.

**II.10:** Ache returns north on Harun's boat, without commanding the vessel, carrying a limited letter and hoping to speak with those who know his family. His answer to refusal is to learn why. This promises a confrontation with home and its people, not a triumphant naval reconquest.

**III.1:** Ache finds that his mother's house has continued through others' work. Amaya says the mother ordered the old red post taken down, the hall kept open and the roof repaired. Crucially, she says the mother **left the house** and refuses to answer publicly where she went. Ache resolves to ask who else heard her instructions. Sulad returns independently to net work, refuses to claim the house for Ache, and agrees to testify only to what he saw.

**III.2:** The women who worked in the hall corroborate the mother's instructions and defend their own claims. Ache receives an answer about the house's use, **not about the mother's whereabouts**. Sulad's independent net work continues.

**III.9–10:** Ache accepts he cannot promise other people's boats, paths, labor or safe passage. Amaya publicly names the unpaid cloth. In III.10 he keeps Tesa's work, and a small safe passage is achieved before rising water closes it. The core ending's last sentence has Ache check the sun before answering a call for a carrier. His mother has not received a direct answer, and Sulad has no equivalent final exchange.

**Bonus III.11–12:** Later history raises the political scale through Spanish contact, then Ache's death and Awi's household perspective. III.12 remembers the mother's prayers and bowl. These memories cannot substitute for a core-book answer to the question III.1 posed about her departure.

## Editorial judgment

**P0 candidate: emotional promise without a core payoff.** The missing mother is more than a missing cast appearance. I.10's undelivered message, II.10's return, and III.1's deliberate withholding make her an active narrative question. By III.10 the text resolves the meaning of her instructions for the household but does not resolve *why she left, whether Ache knows, or whether he chooses to stop seeking an answer*. An unexplained absence can be powerful, but the protagonist must experience its consequence rather than the manuscript simply stop asking.

**P1: Sulad's arc has an established independent desire but no core-ending response.** I.10 corrects Ache's speaking for him. III.1–2 give Sulad independent work and a clear refusal to defend Ache's inherited claims. His absence from III.9–10 may be deliberate, but the final choice should acknowledge that he has built a life outside Ache's command. A forced reunion or appointment would undo his agency.

**P1: the command motif is coherent but overly procedural.** I.10 moves from counting imagined help to counting real bundles. II.10 has Ache choose to travel on someone else's vessel. III.10 has him refuse to guarantee a landing he cannot certify. This is an intelligible arc of relinquished command. The risk is that the final chapter illustrates the lesson with one more transaction instead of making Ache suffer an emotionally consequential decision.

## Two controlled scene experiments

### Experiment M: the question Ache cannot purchase

**Location:** Late III.9 or early III.10, before the final settlement. **Purpose:** Pay off the III.1 question without inventing the mother's death or location. Ache asks Amaya privately whether she will tell him what she knows. She distinguishes what she witnessed from what she promised the mother not to disclose, if such a promise is established by preceding text. Ache must choose whether to press his inherited status to demand an answer or accept that the women of the household have their own obligations. **Non-negotiable:** Do not assert that Amaya has secret knowledge or that the mother died; either must be verified from the entire manuscript first. If Amaya genuinely does not know, she should say so, and Ache must decide what to do with that ignorance.

**Acceptance:** The reader can state what Ache learned, what he still cannot know, why Amaya acted independently, and what he irrevocably gives up. The scene must alter his next action rather than repeat the moral in dialogue. Reject if it feels like an information-withholding trick.

### Experiment S: Sulad refuses to become the ending's witness

**Location:** Late III.9 or III.10, before the final image. **Purpose:** Return to the first book's consent problem. Ache seeks Sulad's help or testimony concerning the house or landing. Sulad chooses his own work and terms, and Ache lets him go without recasting the refusal as loyalty. **Non-negotiable:** Confirm Sulad's last on-page commitments in III.3–8 before scripting this encounter; do not contradict a journey, injury, promise or departure. Do not appoint him governor or make him speak for a whole shore.

**Acceptance:** Sulad gains a discernible choice independent of Ache, and Ache's response is emotionally harder than a routine agreement about wages. Reject if it duplicates a refusal already dramatized or functions merely as cast roll call.

## Recommended order

1. Audit all published III.3–8 Sulad mentions and all mother's references in III.1–12; build a continuity ledger with chapter, actual statement, witness, and remaining uncertainty.
2. Test **M** first because it closes a question explicitly opened in III.1 and anchored in I.10. Do not make the scene an exposition dump.
3. Test **S** only if the continuity ledger reveals a missing payoff rather than a deliberate, already earned separation.
4. Compare each with the published III.10 ending and the separate wage-sacrifice prototype. The wage prototype may be rejected if either relationship scene yields greater irreversible emotional cost with fewer repeated transactions.
5. Keep all experiments in Studio until scoped review, exact-head CI, independent core-only reader assessment and deployment checks. No self-certified 10/10 score.

**Quality criterion:** The eventual climax should make Ache's altered relationship to power *hurt* and change another person's future. A more explicit lesson, longer chapter or invented historical spectacle is not sufficient.
