Reviewed 10 October 2026, Asia/Manila. Studio PR #24; public Novel and checkpoint unchanged.

**Manuscript revision:** Replaced the sequence in which Ache tells his mother that Sulad *might* help, and Sulad later accuses him of having promised his labor, despite no such promise appearing on the page. That sequence repeated the earlier Sima/organizer argument and gave Sulad an unsupported grievance.

**New action:** Ache finds Sulad and his uncle restoring damaged fishing stakes, asks for a crew, and tries to substitute his own labor. He discovers that finishing the stakes does not mend the net or free Sulad in time. He returns empty-handed; the mother's reaction is wordless. This ties the Chapter 1–2 damage to Chapter 4's political dependence on Tondo's available crews without another explicit lesson.
