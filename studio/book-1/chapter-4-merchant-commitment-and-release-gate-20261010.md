## Concrete continuity correction
The current manuscript does **not** show a merchant booking a crew through Tondo. He asks whether two rowers can be held for tomorrow; the organizer says he will answer in Tondo, and the merchant agrees. Earlier handoffs and the Chapter 4–5 structural review overstate this as a completed booking. It is an inquiry whose negotiation has shifted across the river, a weaker but still significant political sign.

The mother's refusal to exclude Tondo crews is more consequential: Maynila cannot immediately replace their labor, provisions and reliability. Ache's unsuccessful attempt to substitute his work for Sulad's specialized fishing labor makes that material limit visible.

**Editorial decision:** Do not invent a completed contract in the dossier. Before publication, decide whether the existing prospective arrangement earns the outline's demand for a concrete loss of bargaining power, or whether a believable on-page commitment with real cost is needed. Keep Sima's independent two-basket bargain and her refusal to restore Ache's authority. Avoid another explanatory lecture.
