## Title decision
**Preferred reader-facing title: The Second Crossing** (provisional).

The disputed second transfer is a literal crossing whose terms neither party understands the same way. Ache also crosses a political boundary when he speaks for Sima without her consent. The second meaning is earned by her refusal and the closing decision to ask rowers before recording their names.

Other possibilities: **The Uncounted** (centers rowers but makes the bookkeeping motif overly explicit); **What the River Keeps** (evocative but risks repeating maritime metaphors across the trilogy); **Encroachment** (accurate Studio working label, too abstract for a published title).

Retain **Encroachment** as the functional outline title until the public prose is ready. Do not change chapter metadata, navigation, or links yet. On publication, review the final scene again and propagate the selected title through canonical chapter metadata and navigation without altering the shared Read Aloud template.
