**The scene's active conflict:** Ache asks his mother to release household provisions or gold for a speculative passage. She must protect merchants, rowers and the Maynila households while confronting her son's urgent wish to seek Brunei support.

**Concrete dramatic beats:**
1. The mother measures dwindling household stores while the steward reports that merchants mistook her request for independent crew offers as an order. She admits this tactical error without surrendering political authority.
2. Ache states the actual fare and uncertainty. She refuses to promise public stores for an unnamed landing or reserve space that the trader has not offered.
3. The unpaid first-voyage rower asks for work, but this does not extinguish his claim against the organizer. Mother cannot solve the wage grievance merely by paying the organizer twice.
4. She reveals a limited personal asset linked to Ache's father. This can foreshadow Aganduru's retrospective gold motif, but she does not yet give Ache an unconditional purse.
5. Her conditions: identify carrier and first landing, explain provisioning and a return-message path, disclose any accompanying person's independent consent, and make no promise of Maynila's food or landing to Brunei.
6. Bulkan's responsibilities in Maynila remain live; he is not automatically reassigned as escort. End with Ache returning to negotiate with better questions, still without passage.
