# Book I Chapters 3–4 — Tondo ruler identity: source correction (10 October 2026)

## Evidence and provenance

Ian Christopher Alfonso, “Revisiting the Provenance of the Very Strange 1589 Last Will and Testament of Don Fernando Malang Balagtas,” *Historical Bulletin* 50 (2016), pp. 31–61, esp. 44–48, https://pssc.org.ph/wp-content/pssc-archives/Historical%20Bulletin/PHA%20HB%202016.pdf. Alfonso notes that the alleged 1521 speech attributed to Mahomet-ben-Suleiman by Aganduru Móriz gives an **unnamed** Tondo cousin, and that Aganduru did not disclose his source. Alfonso conditionally reconstructs this cousin as **Malangsi**, not the Sibunao Lacandola documented as Tondo principal in 1571. That identification depends on the disputed Balagtas testament and further genealogical assumptions; it is NOT verified historical fact. The original Aganduru Spanish passage remains unchecked.

The historically documented Sibunao Lacandola belongs to the 1571 record. No verified source establishes that he ruled Tondo in Ache's adolescence. The public Chapter 3 phrase “collecting in Lakandula’s name,” Chapter 4 outline's Lakandula rivalry, and character relations that grade Ache–Lakandula youthful cousinship as strong inference therefore overstate the evidence.

## Narrative correction gate

In Chapter 3 replace the named authority with “Tondo’s ruler” while preserving Ache's suspicion. In Chapter 4 treat Tondo's ruler/kinsman as an unnamed, possibly related rival and keep the clerk/merchant's agency; do not automatically substitute Malangsi. In the Lakandula profile distinguish the **1571 documented ruler** from the **early unidentified opponent**; remove the strong-inference cousin relationship in Ache's character metadata pending evidence. Review any linked timeline, outline, and character pages. These are retrospective continuity corrections, independent of the Chapter 4 forward checkpoint. No mainline edit is claimed here.

**Chapter 4 title review:** *The Second Crossing* remains provisionally appropriate. No renaming required by this source finding.
